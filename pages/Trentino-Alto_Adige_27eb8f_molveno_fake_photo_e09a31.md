## Introduction

The Molveno lake UFO photo is best understood not as a serious [Trentino-Alto Adige]({{'trentino-alto-adige/' | relative_url }}) sighting, but as a local example of how a fabricated web story can borrow a beautiful place, a dramatic image and a newspaper-like name to look more credible than it is. In October 2016, local media reported that an image claiming to show an alien spacecraft over Lake Molveno was spreading on social media. The claim was quickly identified as false: it came from a fake-news site called “Fatto quotidaino”, a name designed to resemble the established Italian newspaper *Il Fatto Quotidiano* while adding a small spelling change.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ildolomiti.it/societa/da-orson-welles-allufo-sopra-molveno-quando-il-fake-diventa-virale" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ildolomiti.it">[il Dolomiti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ildolomiti.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">il Dolomiti Da Orson Welles all&#x27;ufo sopra Molveno: quando il &quot;fake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo sopra Molveno: quando il &quot;fake&quot; diventa virale. Da ieri sui... lo splendido lago di Molveno, la preziosa perla in più prezioso...Re...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Trentino-Alto_Adige_27eb8f_molveno_fake_photo_e09a31-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Molveno Fake" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
That makes the case useful for Trentino-Alto Adige’s UFO history in a negative but important way. It shows the difference between an official unidentified report, a weakly documented local claim, and an invented story that gains attention because people share the headline before checking the source. Molveno matters here not because an unexplained craft was photographed, but because the episode demonstrates how easily a scenic Alpine setting can be turned into viral UFO folklore.

## How the lake story spread online

The story appeared around 17–20 October 2016, when local Trentino outlets reported that a supposed photograph of a spacecraft above Lake Molveno was circulating on Facebook and other social networks. The headline presented the picture as a “shock” photo of an alien spacecraft over the lake, while the accompanying text claimed that Molveno would now be famous for an alien sighting that would attract attention from experts around the world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ildolomiti.it/societa/da-orson-welles-allufo-sopra-molveno-quando-il-fake-diventa-virale" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ildolomiti.it">[il Dolomiti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ildolomiti.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">il Dolomiti Da Orson Welles all&#x27;ufo sopra Molveno: quando il &quot;fake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo sopra Molveno: quando il &quot;fake&quot; diventa virale. Da ieri sui... lo splendido lago di Molveno, la preziosa perla in più prezioso...Re...</span></span></span>

The setting helped the claim travel. Lake Molveno is already a highly photogenic place: official and tourism sources describe it as an Alpine lake framed by the Brenta Dolomites, promoted for swimming, boating, walking and mountain views.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.visitpaganella.it/en/places-to-explore/molveno" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: visitpaganella.it">[https://www.visitpaganella.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">visitpaganella.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on visitpaganella.it.</span></span></span> A dramatic object added to such a landscape is instantly shareable. It does not need much detail to work as a social-media post: the location is recognisable, the image is striking, and the headline tells readers what reaction to have.

Local reporting also noted that people shared the story in different ways. Some treated it jokingly; others appeared to share it without reading closely. That distinction matters. A fake UFO story can spread even when many participants do not fully believe it. Humour, curiosity and outrage can all push the same false item through a network, and later readers may encounter it without the original wink or context.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giornaletrentino.it/cronaca/trento/dilaga-sul-web-la-foto-dell-ufo-sopra-il-lago-ma-%C3%A8-falsa-1.992265" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giornaletrentino.it">[Trentino]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giornaletrentino.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Trentino Dilaga sul web la foto dell’Ufo sopra il lago. The article did not enter the serious UFO record in the way an official sighting might. Italy’s Air Force explains that its unidentified-object process involves a report submitted through the Carabinieri, technical checks for human or natural explanations, and classification as unidentified only where no technical or natural justification is found after investigation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span> The Molveno photo, by contrast, was a media fabrication exposed by local journalism, not a checked observation by a witness, pilot, radar station or public authority.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Trentino-Alto_Adige_27eb8f_molveno_fake_photo_e09a31-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Molveno Fake illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Trentino-Alto_Adige_27eb8f_molveno_fake_photo_e09a31-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Trentino-Alto_Adige_27eb8f_molveno_fake_photo_e09a31-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## The fake-newspaper problem

The key clue was not hidden in the sky. It was in the source.

The false story came from “Fatto quotidaino”, not *Il Fatto Quotidiano*. Local coverage stressed that the extra letter was not a typo but part of the deception: the site’s name was close enough to a real newspaper brand to catch inattentive readers, while remaining different.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ildolomiti.it/societa/da-orson-welles-allufo-sopra-molveno-quando-il-fake-diventa-virale" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ildolomiti.it">[il Dolomiti]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ildolomiti.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">il Dolomiti Da Orson Welles all&#x27;ufo sopra Molveno: quando il &quot;fake</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo sopra Molveno: quando il &quot;fake&quot; diventa virale. Da ieri sui... lo splendido lago di Molveno, la preziosa perla in più prezioso...Re...</span></span></span> This is a common fake-news tactic. The fake item does not need to persuade a careful reader for long; it only needs to look plausible for the few seconds it takes someone to click, react or share.

The Molveno story also used familiar credibility props:

* **A famous place:** Lake Molveno gave the story a concrete local anchor.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.trentino.com/en/highlights/nature-and-landscape/lakes/lake-molveno/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: trentino.com">[trentino.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">trentino.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Lake Molveno</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lake Molveno</span></span></span>
* **A dramatic headline:** The claim promised a spectacular alien appearance rather than a cautious unidentified object.
* **A pseudo-witness:** Local reporting noted that the text included a named but apparently invented witness, presented as a local 56-year-old resident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.giornaletrentino.it/cronaca/trento/dilaga-sul-web-la-foto-dell-ufo-sopra-il-lago-ma-%C3%A8-falsa-1.992265" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: giornaletrentino.it">[Trentino]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">giornaletrentino.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Trentino Dilaga sul web la foto dell’Ufo sopra il lago. Ma è falsa</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">TrentinoDilaga sul web la foto dell’Ufo sopra il lago. Ma è falsa - Trento - Trentino...</span></span></span>
* **A newspaper-like wrapper:** The imitation outlet made the story look more official than a random social-media post.

This is why the episode is more than a trivial prank. It shows how a fabricated UFO claim can borrow the surface features of journalism without doing journalism. UNESCO’s handbook on journalism, fake news and disinformation treats verification and source scrutiny as central to resisting false information online, and the Molveno case is a small local example of exactly that problem.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/journalism-fake-news-disinformation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: unesco.org">[UNESCO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">unesco.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Journalism, Fake News &amp; Disinformation &#124; UNESCO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Journalism, Fake News &amp; Disinformation &#124; UNESCO</span></span></span>

The fake-newspaper mechanism is especially risky for regional UFO history. Local UFO stories are often preserved through newspaper clippings, community memory and later online retellings. If a false article is copied into blogs, image galleries or social posts without the correction, it can become detached from its original debunking. Years later, readers may find only the picture and the claim, not the explanation that the source was fake.

## Why this was not a serious sighting

A credible UFO case usually begins with a reportable observation: who saw the object, when and where it was seen, how long it lasted, what direction it moved, whether other witnesses or instruments confirmed it, and whether ordinary explanations were checked. The Italian Air Force’s own reporting form asks for details such as date, local time, witness information, and any supporting photographs or film.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

The Molveno case fails at a much earlier stage. The problem was not that the object remained mysterious after investigation. The problem was that the story itself came from a fabricated source, with a fabricated presentation and a fake witness element. Local media did not report an official inquiry, [pilot report]({{'pilot-report/' | relative_url }}), radar trace, police file or independent witness cluster. **Check the outlet before the object.** A tiny spelling difference in a publication name can be the whole trick. In this case, “Fatto quotidaino” was not the real newspaper it resembled. ## What Molveno teaches about regional UFO folklore

The Molveno photo is a cautionary case for the whole Trentino-Alto Adige UFO branch. The region has some official and semi-official UFO material, but it also has modern online scares that look much stronger in a social feed than they do under scrutiny. The Molveno story belongs firmly in the second category.

Its lesson is not that all local UFO reports are fake. That would be too broad and unfair. The better lesson is that the evidential ladder matters. At one end are official reports with dates, witness channels and technical checks. In the middle are local sightings that may be sincere but poorly documented. At the other end are fabricated stories using invented witnesses and imitation news branding. Molveno is valuable because it marks that far end clearly.

It also warns against a common mistake in UFO folklore: preserving the exciting claim and losing the correction. A fake photo can outlive the article that debunked it. A reposted image can become “that UFO over Lake Molveno” long after the fake-news site behind it is forgotten. For a public-facing regional history, the responsible approach is to include the case, but to label it accurately: not an unexplained Molveno sighting, not a hidden Alpine encounter, but a debunked viral fake that shows how easily local scenery can be recruited into UFO mythology.

      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });

      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }

        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }

      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
