## Introduction

A [Molise]({{ 'why-molise-has-so-few-strong-ufo-cases/' | relative_url }}) UFO story becomes an official case only when it enters Italy’s formal reporting system. A newspaper article, social-media video or account sent to a private UFO group does not qualify by itself. The witness must complete the Italian Air Force’s reporting form and deliver it to a Carabinieri station, which forwards the material to the Air Force’s General Security Department. Officials then compare the account with known aircraft activity, weather, astronomical phenomena and other possible causes. Only when those checks fail to produce a technical or natural explanation is the event retained as an unidentified flying object report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Molise_7c6aa1_molise_ufo_reporting_a8691d-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Reporting Process" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

This distinction helps explain an apparent contradiction in Molise’s history. Local media have described several unusual lights and photographs, yet national figures for 1972–2013 credited the region with only one officially registered case. That figure measures reports which completed the institutional process, not every strange object ever reported over Molise.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ilcentro.it/index.php/abruzzo/ufo-diciotto-avvistamenti-in-abruzzo-dal-1972-al-2013-video-1.366865" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ilcentro.it">[il Centro]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ilcentro.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo diciotto avvistamenti in abruzzo dal 1972 al 2013 video 1.366865</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo pervenute all&#x27;Aeronautica militare: 53 dal 1972 a tutto il 2013 su un totale di 433 avvistamenti...</span></span></span>

## Submitting a report through the Carabinieri

Italy’s modern procedure dates from the large national wave of sightings in 1978, after which the government assigned the Italian Air Force responsibility for collecting, checking and monitoring reports. The work is now handled by the General Security Department of the Air Force Staff, whose wider role concerns airspace, flight safety and national security rather than proving or disproving extraterrestrial visitation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare...</span></span></span>

For a witness in Campobasso, Termoli, Isernia or elsewhere in Molise, the formal route begins with the Air Force questionnaire. The completed document must be taken to the nearest Carabinieri station. The Carabinieri act as the local receiving channel and forward it to the Air Force; they are not merely being asked to record an informal complaint or endorse the witness’s interpretation. An Air Force account of its archive says reports from citizens are registered when they arrive through Carabinieri stations so that the information can be compared with known events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

The five-page form is designed to turn an impression such as “a bright sphere crossed the sky” into information that can be checked. It asks for:

* the exact date, local time and duration;

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the observer’s position and viewing direction;
* cloud, wind, rain, fog and visibility conditions;
* the object’s initial and final positions above the horizon;
* apparent speed, direction and manner of movement;
* shape, colour, brightness, sound and apparent size;
* sketches of both the object and its path;
* the names or number of other witnesses;
* details of photographs or film, including the camera and lens where known.

</div>

The instructions tell witnesses to record only details they remember with certainty. They may attach original or copied photographs, recordings and other material that could help officials understand the observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

This level of detail matters because most lights in the sky offer few reliable clues about distance. A point of light that seems low and close might instead be a much brighter object many kilometres away. Without a precise time, direction and duration, investigators may be unable to compare the sighting with a flight, balloon release, planet or meteor. A dramatic description is therefore less useful than a modest but accurately timed one.

## From a Molise report to an Air Force file

Once the submission reaches the Air Force, it becomes a report available for technical assessment. It has not yet become an unresolved UFO case. The official procedure describes an investigation aimed at finding a correlation with human activity or natural phenomena, with other competent bodies involved when necessary. The Air Force says the purpose is flight and national security.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare...</span></span></span>

The published archive tables indicate the practical shape of that assessment. Entries normally condense the witness account into fields such as place, date, time, shape, colour, speed, direction, estimated height, weather and source of the report. A concluding finding states whether the event could be associated with known activity or another recognised phenomenon.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1991-2000.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militarenr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">localita&#x27; data orario forma colore velocita&#x27; direzione moto...May 11, 2023 — SULLA BASE DEI DATI RACCOLTI PRESSO GLI ENTI PREPOSTI, NON...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 11, 2023</span></span></span>

The likely checks fall into several connected groups.

**Aircraft and controlled airspace.** Investigators can ask whether civil or military aircraft were operating in the reported area and time window. Historical Air Force records include cases considered probably attributable to an ordinary aircraft approaching an airport, showing that conventional aviation is treated as a primary possibility rather than an afterthought.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1972-1990.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militarenr. localita&#x27; data orario forma colore velocita&#x27; direzione</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militarenr. localita&#x27; data orario forma colore velocita&#x27; direzione</span></span></span>

**Balloons and radiosondes.** Weather balloons and instrument packages can appear bright, metallic or oddly shaped, particularly when sunlight reaches them after the ground is in shadow. Recent archive findings explicitly state when officials could not associate a sighting with either flight activity or radiosonde operations, demonstrating that these are among the categories checked before an unidentified classification is retained.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/allegato_OVNI_202301.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">allegato OVNI 202301</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitarelocalitaLOCALITA&#x27; DATA ORARIO FORMA COLORE VELOCITA&#x27; 02/02/2023 12:28 LOCALI CORPO SIMILE AD UN PALLONE AEROSTATICO C...</span></span></span>

**Astronomical and atmospheric causes.** Bright planets, meteors and unusual illumination can produce sincere reports. ## What “official UFO” actually means

In the Italian system, the label signifies an unsuccessful identification, not a conclusion about origin. The Air Force states that an episode is classified as an unidentified flying object when the completed checks have not produced a technical or natural explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare...</span></span></span>

That leaves three ideas which are often wrongly merged:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--step-flow" markdown="1">

1. **A reported sighting** is something a person says they observed.
2. **A formally submitted report** is an account delivered through the Carabinieri for Air Force assessment.
3. **An official unidentified case** is a submitted event for which the available checks did not establish a recognised cause.

</div>

The archive’s standard conclusion usually says that no correlation emerged from information gathered from the relevant authorities. It does not claim that every conventional explanation was scientifically eliminated, nor that the object displayed technology beyond known aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1991-2000.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militarenr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">localita&#x27; data orario forma colore velocita&#x27; direzione moto...May 11, 2023 — SULLA BASE DEI DATI RACCOLTI PRESSO GLI ENTI PREPOSTI, NON...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 11, 2023</span></span></span>

This is particularly important for Molise because a single unexplained entry can easily appear more impressive than it is. The regional total says nothing by itself about witness reliability, recording quality, duration or the number of checks performed. A case can remain unidentified simply because the available data do not support a confident answer.

## Why local stories may vanish from official totals

Several points along the reporting chain can prevent a Molise sighting from appearing in national statistics.

**The witness never files the form.** A person may contact a newspaper, post a video or tell a local investigator without visiting the Carabinieri. In that situation the Air Force may receive nothing to assess. The local story exists, but no official file is created through the stated procedure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

**The report arrives too late or lacks usable detail.** Memories of direction, duration and position become less reliable with time. A video without its original file, exact timestamp or known camera orientation may be difficult to compare with aircraft and astronomical data. The form’s detailed questions show how strongly the process depends on information that is often absent from brief press reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

**The event is associated with a known cause.** A report that proves compatible with an aircraft or another recognised phenomenon no longer meets the Air Force’s definition of an unidentified case. It may have passed through official channels without contributing to a published total of unresolved sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare...</span></span></span>

**Several accounts may describe one event.** Multiple witnesses do not necessarily produce multiple official cases. If several reports concern the same time, area and phenomenon, investigators can treat them as evidence concerning one incident rather than count each observer separately. Published tables are organised around events, not the total number of people who saw them. This is an inference from the archive structure rather than an explicitly published counting rule.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1991-2000.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militarenr</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">localita&#x27; data orario forma colore velocita&#x27; direzione moto...May 11, 2023 — SULLA BASE DEI DATI RACCOLTI PRESSO GLI ENTI PREPOSTI, NON...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 11, 2023</span></span></span>

## Why Molise’s total should be read cautiously

Molise’s one-case figure is best understood as an administrative outcome. It records the narrow end of a funnel: observation, formal submission, transmission, investigation and unresolved classification. Every stage can reduce the number that appears in the official archive.

The figure therefore cannot support the strong claim that only one unusual aerial event occurred in Molise between 1972 and 2013. Nor does it establish that the region’s other publicised sightings were false. It shows only that one event was represented in the official figures used by contemporary reporting. Without the associated case papers, it is not safe to identify which later Molise press story, if any, corresponds to that entry.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ilcentro.it/index.php/abruzzo/ufo-diciotto-avvistamenti-in-abruzzo-dal-1972-al-2013-video-1.366865" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ilcentro.it">[il Centro]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ilcentro.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo diciotto avvistamenti in abruzzo dal 1972 al 2013 video 1.366865</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo pervenute all&#x27;Aeronautica militare: 53 dal 1972 a tutto il 2013 su un totale di 433 avvistamenti...</span></span></span>

A small region may also produce fewer reports simply because it has fewer potential observers. Yet population alone cannot explain the archive. Public awareness, willingness to approach the Carabinieri, local media habits and the preservation of original evidence all influence whether a sighting completes the process. These are plausible contributors rather than factors quantified by the Air Force’s published regional statistics.

The practical lesson is that Molise’s official UFO record is not a census of everything unusual seen in its skies. It is a record of the small number of reports that entered a defined state procedure and remained unexplained after the checks that could be made.

## What gives a new report the best chance of being assessed

A witness cannot guarantee that a sighting will remain unidentified, but careful reporting can make the official decision more meaningful. The strongest submission would preserve the original photograph or video, record the time to the nearest minute, mark the viewing direction and angle above the horizon, describe weather and visibility, and obtain separate accounts from other observers before they compare memories.

It is equally useful to note what was not seen: no navigation lights, no engine sound, no visible trail or no change in brightness. Negative details can help distinguish aircraft, meteors and balloons, provided they are recorded accurately rather than added later.

The form should be submitted promptly through a Carabinieri station, with uncertainty stated openly. An observer who remembers the direction but not the apparent size should say so. The official questionnaire expressly asks witnesses to provide only details recalled with certainty, recognising that invented precision makes an investigation less reliable, not more persuasive.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

For Molise’s regional history, this procedure marks the dividing line between a memorable local mystery and an official unidentified case. Publicity can make a sighting famous, but only a documented submission, technical comparison and unresolved finding can give it a lasting place in the Italian Air Force record.
