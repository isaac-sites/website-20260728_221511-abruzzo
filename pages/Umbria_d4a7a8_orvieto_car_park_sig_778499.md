## Introduction

The Orvieto car park sighting is one of the more memorable small cases in [Umbria]({{'umbria/' | relative_url }})’s UFO record because it has a compact, vivid witness story: two women talking in a restaurant car park, a large round shape, zig-zag movement, five steady beams of light and no reported sound. The case was described publicly in 2010 by Andrea Bovo of the Italian Centre for UFO Studies’ Terni group, during coverage of a national ufology meeting in Terni. The strongest reading is cautious: it is a striking witness report, not a verified extraordinary event. Its value lies in showing how a local Umbrian sighting can contain unusually specific details while still lacking the technical information needed for a firm conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Orvieto" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
## The restaurant car park report

The Orvieto incident was reported as having taken place on 13 March 2010 in the Terni province area. According to the published account, two women were speaking in the car park of a restaurant when they saw a very large round outline appear. The object was said to move in a zig-zag pattern with sudden changes of direction. The witnesses also described five fixed beams of light and said the object made no noise.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

That is the core of the case. There is no widely available public file, photograph, video, radar record or official Air Force entry attached to this specific Orvieto report in the accessible sources found for this page. The account survives mainly through local reporting of what Bovo said at the Terni event, rather than through a full primary witness statement. That matters because the narrative is clear enough to be interesting, but not complete enough to test in the way a stronger [aviation]({{'aviation/' | relative_url }}) or astronomical case would require.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

Within Umbria’s broader UFO history, Orvieto is not alone in appearing as a place name in sighting catalogues. The National UFO Centre’s Italian case table lists an Orvieto entry during the major Italian wave of October 1954, and another Orvieto-and-Terni entry on 6 January 1997. Those catalogue entries show that Orvieto has appeared more than once in Italian UFO lists, but they do not prove that the 2010 restaurant car park report belongs to a continuous local pattern.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/CasisticaCunItalia1900-2008.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">CasisticaCunItalia1900 2008</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CasisticaCunItalia1900 2008</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Orvieto illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Movement, beams and silence as witness clues

The details that make the Orvieto report stand out are also the details that need the most care. A “large round” shape is a familiar UFO-reporting category, but it is not a measurement. Without angular size, distance, duration and elevation above the horizon, a witness can honestly describe something as large even when the object’s true size and distance are unknown. The Italian Air Force’s own reporting form asks for exactly these missing anchors: time, weather, observer position, duration, start and end positions, height above the horizon, distance, movement, noise, luminosity, shape and any photographs or video.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/ModuloUFO-1.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

The zig-zag movement is the most dramatic element. If accurate, sudden direction changes would make ordinary aircraft less likely. But visual impressions of direction can be affected by darkness, lack of reference points, changing brightness, movement of the observer, or misjudging whether several lights belong to one object. In the Orvieto account, the available summary does not give a sky map, compass direction, length of observation, weather conditions or comparison with known air traffic.

The five steady beams are equally intriguing but ambiguous. “Beams” could suggest a structured object with lights, but from a distance a witness may also describe glare, reflections, searchlights, aircraft lighting, lanterns, drones, or grouped light sources as beams. Silence narrows some possibilities, especially a low, close helicopter or aircraft, but it does not eliminate distant aircraft, balloons, lanterns, some drones at distance, or optical effects. The important point is not to dismiss the witnesses, but to separate sincere observation from confirmed interpretation.

## Why this case matters in Umbria

The Orvieto report matters because it sits in the middle ground of Umbrian UFO history. It is stronger than a vague “light in the sky” anecdote because it has two witnesses, a named town, a specific setting and distinctive reported behaviour. It is weaker than the best official or multi-instrument cases because the public record does not include independent measurements, official technical checks, photographs, video, radar data or a complete witness questionnaire.

That middle ground is typical of many regional UFO cases. The Italian Air Force states that its institutional role in UFO reporting began after the 1978 wave, with reports collected, checked and monitored for flight safety and national security. It classifies an episode as unidentified only when no technical or natural explanation can be found after checks. The Orvieto restaurant car park case, as publicly available, is not presented in the same evidential form as an official Air Force case file.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span>

The case also helps explain why local UFO groups matter. Regional investigators and enthusiasts often preserve accounts that would otherwise disappear from public memory. In the TuttOggi report, Bovo and Massimo Valloscuro are described as responsible for CISU Terni documentation of a small number of Umbrian sightings from the 1970s onwards. That local preservation is useful, but it does not remove the need for independent corroboration.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Orvieto illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Umbria_d4a7a8_orvieto_car_park_sig_778499-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## What would strengthen or weaken the Orvieto claim

The Orvieto sighting would become more persuasive if additional evidence showed that the two witnesses independently gave consistent descriptions before discussing the event in detail. A full report would ideally include the exact restaurant or car park, time of night, viewing direction, duration, weather, Moon position, visible stars, apparent size, angular movement, and whether any other people nearby saw the same thing.

The strongest supporting material would be independent evidence from outside the witnesses’ memories: photographs, video, separate witness statements from other locations, air-traffic checks, police or Carabinieri reporting, meteorological data, or comparison with known aircraft, lantern releases, events, drones, balloons or advertising lights. This is why modern UAP research places so much emphasis on calibrated, multi-source data rather than witness testimony alone. NASA’s UAP study team found that analysis is often hampered by poor calibration, lack of multiple measurements, missing metadata and lack of baseline data.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: science.nasa.gov">[NASA Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">science.nasa.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Science Independent Study Team Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Science Independent Study Team Report</span></span></span>

The claim would be weakened if the date, time and location matched an ordinary source: a local event using lights, a balloon or lantern release, a drone flight, distant aircraft lights, searchlights, or a misread celestial object. None of those [explanations]({{'explanations-614a80/' | relative_url }}) is confirmed for Orvieto from the available public evidence. They are simply the kinds of checks that should be made before treating a dramatic sighting as unexplained in any strong sense.
