## Introduction

The police material around the Zanfretta case gives the Torriglia story more historical substance than a typical unsupported encounter claim, but it does not independently confirm an alien craft or beings. After security guard Pier Fortunato Zanfretta’s reported experience near Marzano on the night of 6–7 December 1978, the Carabinieri recorded the incident, examined the location and gathered reports of unusual lights from residents in the wider Torriglia and Propata area. A formal information report was later sent to the Genoa court authorities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

That official involvement matters because it shows that something was reported promptly and treated as a real incident rather than invented years later. The strongest corroboration concerns Zanfretta’s distress, interrupted radio contact, unusual lights and a patch of flattened grass. The weakest leap is the one most often made in retellings: that dozens of witnesses verified his description of towering non-human figures. They did not. Most reportedly saw lights, glows or large luminous objects, often from different places and at uncertain times.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Fortunato_Zanfretta" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span></span></span>

## What the Carabinieri actually documented

The first event began during Zanfretta’s night patrol at a property commonly identified as Casa Nostra in Marzano, within the municipality of Torriglia. Believing that moving lights around the grounds might indicate burglars, he left his vehicle and approached. During the ensuing radio exchange, his control room reportedly heard him cry out that those confronting him were “not men”, after which contact was lost. Colleagues Walter Lauria and Raimondo Mascia went looking for him and later described finding him confused and physically distressed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

The owner of Zanfretta’s security company notified the Carabinieri on 7 December. Brigadier Antonio Nucchi subsequently assembled an information report whose title referred to the sighting of unidentified flying objects and humanoids by Fortunato Zanfretta. Accounts reproduced by journalist Rino Di Stefano state that Nucchi forwarded it to the unified Genoa magistrates’ court on 3 January 1979.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.scribd.com/document/619764227/Il-Caso-Zanfretta-La-Vera-Storia-Di-Un-Incredibile-Fatto-Di-Cronaca-Rino-Di-Stefano-Z-lib-org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: it.scribd.com">[Scribd]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">it.scribd.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">T® # Anno..... 79..... This is the documentary core behind the repeated description of Zanfretta as Italy’s “best-documented” abduction case. There was an identifiable officer, a named police station, a dated report and eventual judicial handling. That is considerably better than a story resting solely on a witness’s later memoir or a UFO investigator’s notes.

However, the phrase “police report” is sometimes misunderstood. The Carabinieri were recording statements and circumstances surrounding an unusual complaint. Their involvement did not amount to a scientific finding that a spacecraft had landed. Nor does forwarding a dossier to judicial authorities indicate that prosecutors accepted its extraordinary interpretation. The case was reportedly closed on 11 January 1980 because the material disclosed no basis for a criminal offence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Fortunato_Zanfretta" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span></span></span>

The distinction is important: the authorities documented a reported event; they did not certify its alleged extraterrestrial cause.

## The 52 statements around Torriglia and Propata

Brigadier Nucchi is widely reported to have collected 52 statements concerning strange events in the municipalities of Torriglia and Propata. Later summaries often compress this into the claim that “52 witnesses saw Zanfretta’s UFO”. The surviving public accounts support a more cautious description. The statements concerned unusual lights, glows or large luminous objects in the area, not 52 direct views of the encounter at Casa Nostra.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

This difference changes the evidential value considerably. A witness who sees a bright light beyond a ridge may corroborate that an aerial or atmospheric light was present, but not its distance, size or physical nature. The same testimony cannot establish that the light landed at Marzano, caused the ground mark or contained the beings described by Zanfretta.

Public retellings also provide too little detail to treat the number 52 as a clean scientific dataset. For each statement, a careful assessment would need to know:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* the exact time and date of the observation;
* the witness’s location and viewing direction;
* whether the account was given before or after extensive press coverage;
* whether witnesses discussed the incident with one another;
* how long the object was visible;
* weather, visibility and cloud conditions;
* whether aircraft, planets, vehicles or lights on distant slopes were checked;
* and how many reports referred to the first night rather than later episodes.

</div>

The available summaries do not consistently supply that information. One source describes the 52 accounts as reports of “strange events” gathered across Torriglia and Propata, while popular versions often present them as simultaneous observations of one enormous object. That inflation from a collection of heterogeneous statements into a single mass sighting is not justified by the publicly accessible evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

Even so, the statements should not simply be dismissed. Their presence suggests that Zanfretta’s report arose during a period when several local people believed they had seen unusual lights. This gives the case a regional setting beyond one man’s testimony. It remains possible that some witnesses observed the same phenomenon, particularly where times and directions matched. What is missing is the detailed comparative record needed to demonstrate that match independently.

## Light reports do not equal creature reports

The most important evidential separation is between three levels of claim.

**First, Zanfretta’s condition was reportedly observed by other people.** His colleagues said that radio contact ended abruptly and that they found him in a confused, frightened or feverish state. Those observations support the conclusion that he underwent a distressing episode. They do not identify what caused it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Fortunato_Zanfretta" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span></span></span>

**Second, residents reported unusual illumination.** Some accounts place a strong glow in the general direction of the incident, while others refer more broadly to large luminous objects over Torriglia or Propata. These reports potentially support the presence of an unusual visual stimulus in the district. Their value depends heavily on timing, geometry and independence, details that are rarely reproduced in modern summaries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

**Third, the beings were described principally by Zanfretta.** The police collection is not evidence that dozens of residents saw three-metre-tall figures with unusual eyes and skin. The public summaries of the witness file concentrate overwhelmingly on lights. No comparably large body of independent testimony confirms the appearance, behaviour or number of the alleged entities.

This is where the case’s reputation can mislead. “Corroborated” is accurate only in a restricted sense: other people corroborated parts of the surrounding circumstances. They reportedly confirmed the broken radio exchange, Zanfretta’s distressed condition and unusual lights in the wider area. They did not independently reproduce the central close-encounter narrative.

## The ground mark at Casa Nostra

The Carabinieri reportedly recorded a semicircular area of flattened grass at the property, described in later accounts as roughly two metres in diameter and three metres in length. Because an official officer noted it, the mark is more significant historically than a trace reported only after journalists arrived.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

Yet the mark had little diagnostic value. Flattened vegetation can result from people, vehicles, animals, stored materials, weather or earlier activity at a site. Publicly available descriptions do not establish that the grass had been subjected to unusual heat, pressure, radiation or chemical change. There is no widely available laboratory report demonstrating an anomalous physical process.

Nor does the reported shape provide a secure link to Zanfretta’s object. A semicircular or horseshoe-like patch may appear suggestive when placed beside a UFO narrative, but shape-matching after an event is weak evidence unless investigators first exclude ordinary causes and preserve the scene under controlled conditions. The record shows that a ground disturbance existed; it does not show what created it.

## How independent were the witnesses?

Independence is crucial in a case that rapidly became a national media story. The incident was reported within days by journalists, and Zanfretta later appeared on the popular television programme *Portobello*. Once a striking account circulates, later witnesses may reinterpret earlier ambiguous lights through the new narrative or remember their timing less precisely.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

That does not mean the witnesses were dishonest. Memory is reconstructive, and sincere people can unintentionally align uncertain observations with a highly publicised event. The best statements would therefore be those collected before witnesses knew the details of Zanfretta’s account, with precise times and viewing directions recorded separately.

The surviving public record does not make it easy to divide the 52 statements into early, genuinely independent reports and later reports gathered after publicity began. It also remains unclear how many were first-hand observations, how many concerned the same night and how many related to the wider series of alleged encounters. Because the complete evidential file is not readily available in a searchable public archive, strong claims about unanimity or precise simultaneity should be treated cautiously.

The collection is therefore more valuable as a historical record of a local sighting cluster than as proof of one structured craft. It shows that police received enough reports to create a substantial file. It does not provide the calibrated, time-matched dataset required to triangulate an object or establish its trajectory.

## Nucchi’s later claims

Antonio Nucchi’s role did not end with collecting statements. In later television reporting, after his retirement, he said that he too had witnessed an impressive unidentified aerial phenomenon and had been advised by superiors not to discuss it publicly. This later recollection has become part of the argument that the authorities privately regarded the case as extraordinary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Fortunato_Zanfretta" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span></span></span>

It deserves attention but not automatic acceptance. A police officer is a potentially valuable witness because of professional experience in recording incidents, yet rank does not eliminate ordinary problems of perception or memory. The claim also became public decades after the original events, when the Zanfretta story was already culturally established. Without a dated contemporary statement describing Nucchi’s own sighting, the later account cannot be given the same weight as a report written in 1978 or 1979.

The suggestion that he was told to remain silent is similarly difficult to test. It may indicate institutional caution, concern about ridicule or an ordinary instruction not to speculate during an active enquiry. It is not, by itself, evidence of a concealed official finding.

## Why the judicial closure matters

The judicial file reportedly remained open until January 1980, when it was archived for lack of elements indicating a crime. Believers sometimes regard the existence and duration of the file as official validation. A better reading is that the authorities followed an unusual report through the appropriate administrative channel and found no criminal matter to prosecute.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pier_Fortunato_Zanfretta" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pier Fortunato Zanfretta</span></span></span>

The closure did not debunk the sightings. A magistrate deciding that no offence is apparent is not deciding whether an unidentified light was Venus, an aircraft, an atmospheric effect or an unknown vehicle. But neither did the court endorse Zanfretta’s interpretation. The legal outcome was neutral on the central UFO question.

This procedural point helps place the case correctly within [Liguria]({{ 'why-liguria-became-italys-ufo-coast/' | relative_url }})’s UFO history. The Zanfretta affair generated genuine police paperwork and judicial attention, unlike many later regional photographs or social-media sightings. Its official trail confirms that reports were made and investigated. It does not turn the contents of those reports into established facts.

## How strong was the Torriglia corroboration?

The Torriglia material is strongest when used to support a modest conclusion: during the first Zanfretta episode, colleagues encountered a visibly distressed guard, the authorities recorded a disturbance at the site, and numerous people in the surrounding area reported unusual lights or objects. The event was not merely a story reconstructed for television decades later.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zanfretta_UFO_Incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Zanfretta UFO Incident</span></span></span>

It becomes weak when the same material is used to support a much larger claim. The 52 statements are not publicly demonstrated to be 52 independent, simultaneous sightings of the craft at Casa Nostra. The ground mark was not shown to have an extraordinary cause. The judicial file did not affirm an alien encounter. Most importantly, the alleged beings and abduction were not independently observed by the regional witnesses.

A balanced assessment can therefore separate the case into three evidential grades:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Well supported as historical fact:** Zanfretta reported a frightening event; colleagues responded; the Carabinieri investigated; statements were collected; and a report went to the Genoa judicial authorities.
* **Partly corroborated but unresolved:** unusual lights were reportedly seen around Torriglia and Propata, and flattened grass was recorded at the property.
* **Uncorroborated extraordinary interpretation:** the lights were a landed alien craft, the vegetation mark was made by it, and the beings described by Zanfretta were physically present.

</div>

This limited but real corroboration explains why the case remains central to Ligurian UFO history. The official records prevent it from being dismissed as a completely undocumented legend. At the same time, the gap between what the records establish and what later retellings claim is substantial. Torriglia offers evidence of an investigated disturbance and a local cluster of unusual-light reports—not independent confirmation of the creatures or abduction at the heart of the Zanfretta story.
