## Introduction

Matera’s place in [Basilicata]({{'what-really-happened-in-basilicatas-ufo/' | relative_url }})’s UFO history is less a story of one famous, well-proven mystery than of a revealing overlap: a province with scattered sighting reports sits beside one of Italy’s most important space-operation sites. That matters because Matera is a place where ordinary sky events can feel unusually suggestive. Satellites, rocket plumes, space-debris tracking, lanterns, meteors and aircraft all belong in the same interpretive frame as local UFO claims. The strongest conclusion is cautious: Matera province does appear in Italian UFO catalogues and local reporting, but the better-documented modern examples often point towards misidentification or remain too thinly reported to support strong claims. The space-centre backdrop does not “explain away” every report, but it changes how the reports should be read: in Matera, the sky is not just folklore; it is also infrastructure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.asi.it/en/the-agency/the-space-centers/giuseppe-colombo-center-for-space-geodesy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: asi.it">[asi.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">asi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">giuseppe colombo center for space geodesy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Agenzia Spaziale Italiana...</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Matera" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
## Why Matera is a special UFO setting in Basilicata

Matera is unusual within Basilicata because its UFO interest is attached to a real, visible space context. The Italian Space Agency’s Matera centre was established in 1983 in the Murgia Terlecchia area and is described by ASI as its main operational centre. Its historical core is space geodesy: measuring Earth’s shape, movement and reference points using satellite laser ranging, very long baseline radio interferometry, satellite navigation systems and gravity measurement. ASI also says the centre has grown into remote sensing, free-space quantum telecommunications, space-debris tracking, and time and frequency work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.asi.it/en/the-agency/the-space-centers/giuseppe-colombo-center-for-space-geodesy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: asi.it">[ASI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">asi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">giuseppe colombo center for space geodesy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Agenzia Spaziale Italiana...</span></span></span>

That does not mean UFOs reported in Matera are secretly produced by the centre. It means Matera is one of the Italian places where the public’s mental map of the sky is already shaped by space activity. When people know that satellites are being tracked, Earth-observation data are received, and space debris is monitored nearby, unusual lights can acquire an immediate “space” interpretation. The e-GEOS Matera Space Centre, operating for ASI, is tied to Earth-observation data acquisition, processing, archiving and distribution, including the Italian Civil Ground Segment for COSMO-SkyMed and work for ESA satellite missions such as Sentinel-1 in the Copernicus programme.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.telespazio.com/it/business/space-centres-teleports/matera-space-centre" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: telespazio.com">[Telespazio]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">telespazio.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro spaziale di Matera</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro spaziale di Matera</span></span></span>

For Basilicata’s wider UFO record, this makes Matera different from purely rural or mountain sighting locations. Reports from the province are not just local anecdotes; they are filtered through a landscape where satellites are part of the everyday institutional story. That makes the province useful for asking a practical question: when does a strange light remain genuinely unidentified, and when is it only unfamiliar because most witnesses do not know how modern space activity looks from the ground?

## Matera province in sighting lists

Matera province appears in several UFO lists and local summaries, but the evidence is uneven. A Centro Ufologico Nazionale catalogue of Italian sightings from 1900 to 2008 includes a Matera entry in spring 1964 at about 10 pm, recorded only in catalogue form with little public detail attached. The same catalogue also lists the December 1978 wave with Basilicata-related entries including Pisticci before dawn on 20 December, Policoro at night on 21 December, and a Bari-and-Matera entry at dawn on 21 December.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/CasisticaCunItalia1900-2008.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionale</span></span></span>

Those catalogue entries are important historically, but they are not the same as full case files. They give date, place and a coded classification, yet they do not provide enough public information to reconstruct the sighting properly: exact viewing direction, duration, number of witnesses, weather, astronomical conditions, aircraft movements, photographs, radar data or follow-up interviews. For a reader, the right response is neither dismissal nor belief. These entries show that Matera province was part of the Italian ufological record, especially around the 1978 national wave, but they do not by themselves demonstrate extraordinary objects.

Local reporting has also treated the Matera area as one of Basilicata’s recurring sighting zones. A 2018 local summary of regional UFO history, drawing on ufological material, identified the city of Matera, Pisticci and the badlands area among the Matera-side places most associated with reports, and referred to a 1978 Italian “flap”, or peak of sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lagazzettadelmezzogiorno.it/news/home/998605/la-basilicata-e-gli-ufo-una-storia-di-70-anni.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lagazzettadelmezzogiorno.it">[lagazzettadelmezzogiorno.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lagazzettadelmezzogiorno.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">la basilicata e gli ufo una storia di 70 anni</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">la basilicata e gli ufo una storia di 70 anni</span></span></span> Again, that is best read as evidence of a local reporting tradition rather than evidence that the province was a confirmed hotspot in any official sense.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Matera illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## The 1978 wave still shapes the archive

The year 1978 matters because it changed the Italian institutional context. The Italian Air Force says that, following the 1978 wave of sightings, Prime Minister Giulio Andreotti designated the Air Force as the institutional body responsible for collecting, checking and monitoring UFO reports. The Air Force now explains its role in flight-safety and national-security terms: reports are checked for correlations with human activity or natural phenomena, and only after investigation are cases published as unidentified where no technical or natural explanation is found.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span>

Matera province’s December 1978 entries therefore sit at the edge of two histories. On one side, they belong to the broader Italian UFO wave, with multiple reports across the country in a short period. On the other, they help explain why official reporting later became more structured. The CUN catalogue’s Matera-linked December entries are clustered among many national reports from the same few days, which is typical of a “flap”: sightings multiply, media attention rises, and ambiguous lights are more likely to be reported because witnesses are primed to look up.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/CasisticaCunItalia1900-2008.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionale</span></span></span>

That clustering weakens and strengthens the story at the same time. It strengthens it historically because Matera province was not an isolated footnote; it was part of a recognisable Italian sighting wave. But it weakens any claim of a single extraordinary Matera event, because wave conditions can mix genuinely puzzling observations with mistaken reports of meteors, aircraft, planets, lanterns, re-entering debris or distant lights.

## The space geodesy and satellite setting

The Matera centre’s work helps explain why satellite interpretation is especially relevant here. Satellite laser ranging means firing laser pulses at satellites equipped with reflectors and measuring the return to calculate position with great precision. Very long baseline interferometry uses distant radio sources and separated antennas to measure Earth orientation and reference frames. GNSS work uses satellite navigation systems, while remote sensing involves receiving and processing data from Earth-observation satellites. These are highly technical operations, but the public-facing point is simple: Matera is a place where satellites are not abstract. They are tracked, measured, received and used.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.asi.it/en/the-agency/the-space-centers/giuseppe-colombo-center-for-space-geodesy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: asi.it">[ASI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">asi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">giuseppe colombo center for space geodesy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Agenzia Spaziale Italiana...</span></span></span>

The centre’s role also extends into modern satellite infrastructure. e-GEOS says the Matera Space Centre is the main node of the civil infrastructure for COSMO-SkyMed, active since the first satellite launch in 2007, and that it processes and distributes data for several international satellite missions. Since 2014 it has also been one of ESA’s Core Ground Segment stations for near-real-time Sentinel-1 radar data.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.telespazio.com/it/business/space-centres-teleports/matera-space-centre" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: telespazio.com">[Telespazio]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">telespazio.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro spaziale di Matera</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro spaziale di Matera</span></span></span>

For UFO interpretation, this matters in three ways:<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **More space activity means more visible sky triggers.** Satellites, rocket stages and re-entries are now common enough to produce public reports across wide regions.
* **A space-centre identity can frame witness expectations.** In a province known for space operations, a moving light may be interpreted as satellite-related, secret-technology-related or UFO-related faster than in a place without that association.
* **Technical context helps investigation.** A serious case from Matera should be checked against satellite passes, launches, re-entries, aircraft tracks, weather, astronomical objects and local event lighting before being labelled mysterious.</div>

The space-centre backdrop therefore does not make Matera reports less interesting. It makes them more testable.

## When “UFOs over Matera” turned out to be lanterns

The cleanest modern Matera example is not an unresolved case but a solved one. In November 2012, residents in Matera reported bright objects moving in the sky from the area of Via Aldo Moro. Witnesses described strong lights moving together, almost like a formation, and a video was reportedly taken by an engineer who was present with other people. The story initially had the structure of a classic urban UFO report: multiple witnesses, moving lights, apparent formation and uncertainty.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sassilive.it/cronaca/pubblica-utilita/avvistano-ufo-a-matera-ma-sono-lanterne-della-festa-di-cristo-re-guarda-il-video/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sassilive.it">[SassiLive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sassilive.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on sassilive.it.</span></span></span>

The explanation came quickly. A local priest from the Cristo Re parish clarified that the lights were simple lanterns released from the parish square during the feast of Cristo Re. That makes the case valuable precisely because it shows how an impressive-looking report can collapse once local context is restored. Lights that seem to “chase” each other or move in formation can be lightweight objects drifting together in the same air current.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sassilive.it/cronaca/pubblica-utilita/avvistano-ufo-a-matera-ma-sono-lanterne-della-festa-di-cristo-re-guarda-il-video/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sassilive.it">[SassiLive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sassilive.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on sassilive.it.</span></span></span>

This episode is a good warning for Matera province as a whole. The most useful UFO cases are not always the most spectacular ones; they are the ones where enough detail exists to test the claim. In 2012, the decisive evidence was not radar or a scientific instrument. It was local knowledge: the time, place and event matched a release of lanterns.

## The 2024 SpaceX lesson from nearby skies

A newer regional example shows how modern space activity can produce UFO-like reports across southern Italy. On 23 June 2024, thousands of people in Puglia and Basilicata reportedly noticed a strange cloud-like object followed by a star-like point moving at the same speed for several minutes. Local experts identified it not as a UFO but as a SpaceX rocket-related event, and one widely used image was reported from Policoro Lido in the province of Matera.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://bari.repubblica.it/cronaca/2024/06/24/news/avvistamento_cielo_puglia_non_e_ufo_ma_razzo_spacex-423277069/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: bari.repubblica.it">[la Repubblica]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">bari.repubblica.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">la Repubblica Nel cielo in Puglia spunta un oggetto volante non</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">la Repubblica Nel cielo in Puglia spunta un oggetto volante non</span></span></span>

This is exactly the kind of event that matters for Matera. It was not centred on the ASI base, but it was visible in the same regional sky culture: Puglia, Basilicata and the Ionian-facing Matera province. Rocket upper stages can produce strange-looking plumes when propellant is vented and sunlight catches frozen particles high above a dark ground. Similar SpaceX-related plumes have been widely reported across Europe, with skywatching sources explaining that vented propellant from Falcon 9 upper stages can freeze and reflect sunlight, creating diffuse clouds or spiral-like forms.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/stargazing/stargazers-in-europe-spot-a-strange-cloud-from-spacex-falcon-9-rocket-launch" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Stargazers in Europe spot a strange cloud from Space X</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Stargazers in Europe spot a strange cloud from Space X</span></span></span>

For readers, the lesson is practical. A slow, luminous, cloud-like object that appears after sunset or before sunrise, moves steadily, and is seen across a wide area is often more consistent with a rocket plume, satellite deployment or upper-stage activity than with a low-flying craft. Matera’s space-centre backdrop makes that interpretation especially relevant, but the cause can be hundreds or thousands of kilometres away.<img src="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Matera illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Basilicata_3ea395_matera_space_backdro_5d9c75-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## Satellites, trains of lights and public confusion

Not every space-related misidentification is dramatic. Some are simply unfamiliar. Starlink satellites, for example, can appear as a line or “train” of bright points shortly after launch, especially in the first days before they spread out and climb to operational altitude. Public reports often describe these as evenly spaced lights moving silently across the sky, which is why they can be mistaken for coordinated craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.space.com/spacex-starlink-satellites.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: space.com">[Space]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">space.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Starlink satellites: Facts, tracking and impact on astronomy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Starlink satellites: Facts, tracking and impact on astronomy</span></span></span>

The broader orbital environment is also becoming more crowded. Matera’s own institutional role sharpens this point. ASI explicitly includes space-debris tracking among the areas incorporated into the Matera centre’s work over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.asi.it/en/the-agency/the-space-centers/giuseppe-colombo-center-for-space-geodesy/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: asi.it">[ASI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">asi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">giuseppe colombo center for space geodesy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Agenzia Spaziale Italiana...</span></span></span> A province associated with tracking objects in orbit is also a province where members of the public may increasingly see, film and misread objects in orbit.

Recent UFO-themed media has continued to include Matera, but often in a way that underlines uncertainty rather than proof. In 2024, several Italian outlets reported a CUFOM-linked set of cases from Campania, Sicily and Basilicata, including a Matera case described as a swarm of white objects. The important detail is that the case was itself described as doubtful, even if not resolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ottopagine.it/bn/attualita/350424/ufo-due-enormi-sfere-bianche-avvistate-nel-beneventano.shtml" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ottopagine.it">[ottopagine.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ottopagine.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo: due enormi sfere bianche avvistate nel beneventano</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ufo: due enormi sfere bianche avvistate nel beneventano</span></span></span>

## How space activity changes interpretation in Matera province

Matera’s space role does not produce a simple debunking rule. It produces a better checklist. A serious Matera province UFO report should be judged by whether it survives ordinary tests that are especially relevant to this location.

The first test is **timing**. Was the object seen shortly after sunset or before sunrise, when satellites and rocket stages can still be sunlit while observers are in darkness? The second is **motion**. A steady path across the sky suggests a satellite, aircraft or high-altitude object more than a hovering local craft, while a drifting cluster can suggest lanterns, balloons or birds. The third is **geography**. If the same object was reported across Puglia, Basilicata and Calabria, a high-altitude or astronomical explanation becomes more likely than a local Matera object.

The fourth test is **local context**. The 2012 lantern case shows why investigators must ask what was happening on the ground: festivals, religious events, weddings, drones, fireworks, searchlights and aircraft activity can all create reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.sassilive.it/cronaca/pubblica-utilita/avvistano-ufo-a-matera-ma-sono-lanterne-della-festa-di-cristo-re-guarda-il-video/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: sassilive.it">[SassiLive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">sassilive.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on sassilive.it.</span></span></span> The fifth test is **space context**.     var template = String(cfg.url_template ||'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query ||'')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query ||''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ?'&' :'?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id ||'10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label ||'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId ||'').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g,'-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g,'');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded','false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') ||'';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') ||'';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ?'true' :'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ?'true' :'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') ==='1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init','1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey:'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') ==='true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ?'false' :'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
