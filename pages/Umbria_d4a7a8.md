## Introduction

The most important episodes include a strange roadside encounter near [Nocera Umbra]({{ 'nocera-1977/' | relative_url }}) in 1977, a multi-location aerial sighting involving Terni during Italy’s national [1978 flap]({{ '1978-flap/' | relative_url }}), binocular observations at Polino in 2001, and an apparently erratic luminous object reported near Orvieto in 2010. None supplies physical evidence of an extraterrestrial vehicle. Their value lies instead in showing why some reports remain technically unidentified while others weaken once investigators compare testimony with aircraft, balloons, satellites, weather and human-made objects.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

## A regional history with more gaps than landmarks

Modern UFO history in Umbria is fragmented. Many reports first appeared in local newspapers rather than in police, aviation or scientific records, especially before organised civilian cataloguing became established. The Centro Italiano Studi Ufologici, or CISU, began its regional catalogue project in 1981, assembling local case files from press cuttings, witness interviews and investigators’ reports. Its wider national archive now contains many thousands of reports, but a report’s inclusion in a catalogue means only that someone described an unidentified phenomenon, not that investigators confirmed an extraordinary craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cisu.org/ufo-in-italia/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cisu.org">[CISU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cisu.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Ufo in Italia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Iniziato infatti nel 1981, il “Progetto Cataloghi Regionali” ha visto la creazione di archivi locali della casistica ufologica, costantem...</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because published totals vary sharply according to what is counted. A regional news feature claimed dozens of Umbrian reports in individual years such as 2010–12, while an earlier account of CISU work highlighted only four especially notable cases over roughly three decades. The apparent contradiction probably reflects different thresholds: one figure may include every initial notification, whereas another counts only selected, investigated or still-unresolved incidents. Without a public case-by-case regional dataset showing duplicates, solutions and evidential grades, precise totals should be treated cautiously.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.realumbria.it/21/04/2021/oggetti-volanti-non-identificati-umbria/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: realumbria.it">[Real Umbria]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">realumbria.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Real Umbria Oggetti volanti non identificati: avvistamenti eclatanti in Umbria</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Real Umbria Oggetti volanti non identificati: avvistamenti eclatanti in Umbria</span></span></span>

Umbria’s geography also shapes the evidence. Reports come from the skies above Perugia and Terni, but several memorable cases occurred on rural roads or in sparsely populated hill country around Nocera Umbra, [Polino]({{ 'polino/' | relative_url }}), Porano and Lake Trasimeno. Darkness, uneven terrain and a lack of fixed visual references can make distance, size and speed extremely difficult to judge. A bright point of light may seem to stop, dart or change direction when the observer moves, when clouds alter its brightness, or when the eye makes tiny involuntary movements against a dark background.

## Nocera Umbra, 1977: the region’s strangest witness story

The most unusual Umbrian report is not a conventional light in the sky. On the night of 11 August 1977, a railway worker driving home near Nocera Umbra reportedly saw a shining, roughly triangular figure moving along the road in front of his car. Later retellings describe it as metallic-looking, supported by extremely thin legs and travelling in a series of short leaps. When the driver accelerated, the figure supposedly increased the length and frequency of its jumps before turning sharply and disappearing. A contemporary local newspaper was cited at a 2010 UFO conference in Terni as the original documentary source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

The case is memorable because the witness did not merely see a distant point. He described a structured, close-range presence illuminated by his headlights. That kind of report is sometimes classified as a “humanoid encounter”, although the label describes the testimony rather than establishing what was physically present. A later account also mentioned another local man who said he heard a loud whistling sound at about the same time, but the surviving summaries do not demonstrate that the sound and the roadside sighting had a common source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://thinkaboutitdocs.com/1977-august-ufo-alien-sightings/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: thinkaboutitdocs.com">[Think About It Docs]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">thinkaboutitdocs.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">1977 august ufo alien sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Think About It Docs1977: August UFO &amp; Alien Sightings1977: August UFO &amp; Alien Sightings Date: August 1977 Location: Sierra... The evidential weaknesses are substantial. There is no known photograph, trace sample, police file, medical record or surviving object. Most readily accessible descriptions are later summaries rather than the full original interview and newspaper coverage. Even apparently precise details—height, width and jumping distance—may have been estimates reconstructed after a startling night-time encounter. Possible conventional explanations include an animal seen under unusual lighting, a person carrying reflective material, roadside equipment, or a perceptual error amplified by fear and movement. None is demonstrated, but neither is the extraordinary interpretation.

The case therefore remains narratively strong and evidentially weak. It may represent an honest but badly misinterpreted encounter. It may also contain details sharpened through repeated [retelling]({{ 'retelling/' | relative_url }}). Its place in Umbrian UFO history rests on the strangeness of the account, not on corroboration.

## Terni and the national flap of 1978

The clearest official link between Umbria and Italian UFO history occurred on 9 March 1978. An Italian Air Force archive records an elongated red-and-green phenomenon observed between about 8.30 and 8.40 pm from several locations, including Terni, Bologna, the Gran Sasso area, Vicenza and Ancona. The witnesses included military personnel and civilian pilots. The archive states that, after examination of the available material, the event was catalogued as an unidentified flying object.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1972-1990.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

This case matters more than an isolated private report because the observation was geographically widespread and involved aviation witnesses. It also belongs to the exceptional Italian wave of 1978, the year that prompted Prime Minister Giulio Andreotti to designate the Air Force as the national body responsible for collecting, checking and monitoring UFO reports. The Air Force still receives reports through the Carabinieri and investigates whether they correspond to aircraft activity, technical events or natural phenomena. Only reports without an identified explanation after that process are retained as unidentified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/en/2023/01/12/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare...</span></span></span>

Official classification should not be confused with proof of exotic technology. The March record supplies no radar plot, photograph, measured trajectory or detailed meteorological analysis in its published summary. It lists the phenomenon’s general shape and colours but leaves speed, direction, altitude and weather unspecified. The wide viewing area could indicate a high-altitude event visible across central and northern Italy rather than a craft manoeuvring locally over Terni. A re-entering object, distant rocket-related event or unusual astronomical phenomenon would need to be tested against the precise times and viewing directions, which the public table does not provide.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1972-1990.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

The fairest verdict is therefore “officially unresolved in the published archive”. The Air Force record makes the event more historically important than a newspaper anecdote, but its brevity prevents a modern reader from judging why ordinary explanations failed.

## Polino and Orvieto: the better modern witness cases

In August 2001, three people near Polino reportedly watched an oval, star-like light that appeared to move slowly, stop and accelerate. They had binoculars and described a diffuse yellow light together with a more sharply defined red component inside the principal glow. A CISU representative later presented the report as one of Umbria’s unresolved cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

Multiple witnesses and binocular observation improve the case compared with a single fleeting sighting. They reduce the likelihood that the entire episode was invented and suggest that the observers had time to compare impressions. Yet binoculars do not automatically provide reliable distance or size. Hand-held magnification can exaggerate apparent movement, especially when viewing a bright astronomical object with no visible horizon. Atmospheric turbulence can split, distort and colour a bright point, while the “autokinetic effect” can make a stationary light appear to wander when seen against a featureless dark sky.

The public accounts do not provide the exact date, duration, viewing direction, elevation, weather, star map, flight information or original witness drawings needed for a decisive reconstruction. The description could fit an unusual aircraft presentation, a bright celestial object distorted by the atmosphere, or another distant light. It remains unidentified mainly because the available information does not discriminate strongly among these possibilities.

The [Orvieto]({{ 'orvieto/' | relative_url }}) report of 13 March 2010 followed a different pattern. Two women talking in a restaurant car park said they saw a large, round silhouette moving in a zigzag with sudden directional changes. They also described five steady beams of light and no sound. A CISU investigator included it among the region’s unresolved sightings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

This sounds more structured than the Polino light, but the case has the same central problem: no published photograph, independent instrumental record or detailed reconstruction. “Very large” and “silent” are difficult claims to assess without knowing distance. A nearby small object can look like a distant large one, while an aircraft may be inaudible because of wind, terrain, viewing angle or delayed sound. Apparent zigzags can also result from witness movement or a light becoming intermittently obscured. The report is worth preserving, but later coverage has not materially strengthened it.

## Badiale shows why investigation matters


<div class="content-callout content-callout--key content-callout--takeaway" markdown="1">

One of the most instructive Umbrian cases occurred near Badiale, outside Città di Castello, in August 1998. Two agricultural workers reportedly saw an object rise from nearby ground, expand into a cylinder roughly two metres tall, reflect sunlight and drift away. The description initially sounded highly unusual. Investigators later compared it with a commercially available “solar UFO”: a long, lightweight black plastic balloon heated and inflated by the Sun. The case was consequently archived as a non-UFO.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

</div>

This is a valuable regional example because the original witnesses need not have lied or imagined the event. They may have accurately described something unfamiliar. The error lay in identification, not necessarily observation. Solar balloons can inflate, change shape, rise silently and reflect light in ways that appear mechanical or purposeful.

A similar lesson comes from Piediluco in 2002, where flattened barley prompted speculation about a crop circle or landing trace. The farmer himself said the growth had simply lodged after wind and rain and that similar flattening occurred routinely. Local reporting also noted that publicity surrounding UFO talks and television programmes had primed some observers to interpret an ordinary agricultural pattern as evidence of a landing.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.piediluco.info/trovato-crop-in-umbria/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: piediluco.info">[Pro Loco Piediluco]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">piediluco.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pro Loco Piediluco Trovato Crop in Umbria?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pro Loco Piediluco Trovato Crop in Umbria?</span></span></span>

Together, Badiale and Piediluco show that investigation can resolve impressive-looking claims without accusing witnesses of bad faith. They also warn against treating a lack of immediate recognition as evidence of an extraordinary origin.

## Porano, Lake Trasimeno and the problem of unsupported detail

Two driving reports from 2012 gained regional attention. Near Porano on 9 January, a motorist reportedly saw a rotating disc-like structure with a flashing red light and estimated a diameter of at least ten metres. Other people were later said to have seen something similar over Perugia the following evening. Near Tuoro sul Trasimeno in November, a couple returning home described a rotating object above a hill, with steady blue lights fading towards a pinkish colour. No photograph or video was reported in either case, and the Tuoro observation was not independently confirmed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.realumbria.it/21/04/2021/oggetti-volanti-non-identificati-umbria/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: realumbria.it">[Real Umbria]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">realumbria.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Real Umbria Oggetti volanti non identificati: avvistamenti eclatanti in Umbria</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Real Umbria Oggetti volanti non identificati: avvistamenti eclatanti in Umbria</span></span></span>

These accounts contain vivid estimates of rotation, colour and size, but the apparent precision is misleading. A witness cannot reliably calculate the diameter of a night-time object without knowing its distance. A small illuminated object nearby and a large object far away can produce the same angular size. Lights attached to an aircraft, drone, balloon or ground installation can also create an impression of a solid rotating body when the underlying structure is invisible.

Claims that the same object appeared over Perugia a day later should be treated particularly cautiously unless the observations can be matched by time, direction and distinctive behaviour. Similar red lights are common in aviation and telecommunications. Separate witnesses describing broadly similar colours do not by themselves establish a single travelling object.

## Photographs and video have not solved the regional mystery

Digital imagery has added volume to Umbrian UFO reporting but not necessarily clarity. A 2021 Terni case involved photographs of white spherical objects said to have appeared silently for several minutes. A private UFO group reported that the files showed no obvious signs of digital manipulation. That establishes, at most, that the image files may be original; it does not establish the identity, distance or nature of the photographed objects. Unedited photographs can faithfully record balloons, birds, aircraft, insects, lens artefacts or objects too small and blurred to identify.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.umbriajournal.com/curiosita/dischi-volanti-nel-cielo-ufo-uap-avvistanti-sui-cieli-di-terni-401239/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: umbriajournal.com">[umbriajournal.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">umbriajournal.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dischi volanti nel cielo, ufo, UAP, avvistanti sui cieli di Terni</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dischi volanti nel cielo, ufo, UAP, avvistanti sui cieli di Terni</span></span></span>

A further Terni report in June 2022 involved three witnesses who described three objects performing what investigators considered unconventional movements. A local article said informal consultation with people connected to the Air Force had made a training exercise appear unlikely, while parachutists and ordinary light aircraft were also discussed. However, this was not presented as a formal Air Force investigation, and the published account did not include radar data, original footage at full quality, measured angles or a complete flight-traffic check. The investigator himself cautioned against claims of alien craft and acknowledged that a terrestrial explanation might later emerge.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/terni-avvistamento-ufo-indagini-in-corso-video/687899/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[Tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

Modern satellite constellations further complicate public reporting. In May 2021, rows of lights seen across Umbria were correctly identified in local coverage as Starlink satellites rather than UFOs. Starlink spacecraft can appear as striking trains of moving points after launch, and their brightness varies with viewing geometry and reflected sunlight. The episode demonstrates how a spectacular regional “wave” can arise from one widely visible and fully conventional source.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/puntini-luminosi-nel-cielo-dellumbria-ecco-cosa-sono/627492/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">puntini luminosi nel cielo dellumbria ecco cosa sono</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">puntini luminosi nel cielo dellumbria ecco cosa sono</span></span></span>

## No firm military or aviation pattern emerges

Umbria’s 1978 entry is a genuine military-aviation connection because Air Force personnel and civil pilots were among the witnesses. Beyond that, the public evidence does not reveal a sustained pattern involving regional airfields, radar stations or repeated incursions into protected airspace. Later claims sometimes mention consultations with military personnel, but an informal opinion is not equivalent to an official operational record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1972-1990.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

The Italian Air Force’s role is also narrower than popular accounts sometimes suggest. It investigates reports for flight and national security, seeking correlations with human activity and natural phenomena. An “unidentified” result means that the available information did not yield an explanation; it does not represent institutional endorsement of an extraterrestrial hypothesis. The Air Force has also warned that material before 2001 is still being reorganised for publication, so its online archive should not be assumed to contain every historical Umbrian report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/en/2023/01/12/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare...</span></span></span>

This explains why civilian and official histories do not align neatly. Nocera Umbra’s 1977 roadside encounter is prominent in UFO literature but does not appear as an Umbrian entry in the published Air Force archive for 1972–90. Conversely, the official March 1978 multi-location sighting is less colourful than the Nocera story but has stronger institutional provenance. Different archives preserve different kinds of evidence.

## Ancient Spoleto is history, not a modern case file

Some UFO chronologies begin Umbria’s story in antiquity. Julius Obsequens, drawing on earlier Roman material, reported that in the territory of Spoletum a golden “globe of fire” appeared to fall, rise again and move eastwards, becoming bright enough to obscure the Sun. The episode has been discussed in academic work on anomalous aerial reports in classical sources.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_sightings_in_Italy" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO sightings in Italy</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO sightings in Italy</span></span></span>

It is an interesting piece of regional sky folklore, but it cannot be investigated like a modern observation. The text survived through copying and compression; there is no exact location, independent measurement or physical evidence. Ancient writers often treated unusual celestial events as omens, and descriptions of globes, shields and torches could refer to meteors, atmospheric lights, comets or literary symbolism. The account belongs in the cultural prehistory of unidentified aerial phenomena, not in a statistical tally of modern Umbrian UFO sightings.

## What remains unresolved—and what does not

Umbria’s cases fall into three useful categories.

**Reasonably explained:** the 1998 Badiale cylinder was convincingly compared with a solar balloon; the 2002 Piediluco “crop circle” was attributed by the landowner to weather-damaged growth; and the ordered lights seen in 2021 were identified as Starlink satellites. These cases demonstrate that dramatic appearance and sincere testimony can coexist with an ordinary cause.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://tuttoggi.info/ufo-in-umbria-4-avvistamenti-in-30-anni-i-lavori-del-25-convegno-nazionale-ufologia-terni-video-e-foto-tuttoggi-info/85047/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tuttoggi.info">[tuttoggi.info]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tuttoggi.info</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on tuttoggi.info.</span></span></span>

**Unresolved but weakly documented:** Porano, Tuoro sul Trasimeno and Orvieto remain unexplained in the limited public accounts, but they lack the photographs, measurements and independent records needed to favour an exceptional interpretation. “Unresolved” here mostly describes an information gap.

**Historically stronger but still inconclusive:** the 1978 Terni-linked observation benefits from official documentation and aviation witnesses, while Polino benefits from three witnesses and binocular observation. Even these cases lack enough publicly available data for confident identification or for claims about exotic technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Archivio_OVNI_periodo1972-1990.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

The Nocera Umbra encounter sits outside this scale. It is the region’s most extraordinary narrative but one of its hardest cases to evaluate because nearly everything depends on a single witness account preserved through later reporting.

## The balanced verdict on Umbria

Umbria’s UFO history is real as a history of reports, investigations and local belief. It includes one officially catalogued 1978 observation linked to Terni, several persistent civilian cases, and a record of investigators successfully identifying misleading balloons, satellites and weather effects. What it does not contain is publicly verified physical evidence, a confirmed radar-visual incident over the region, or a chain of documentation establishing visits by non-human craft.

Later reporting has generally not strengthened the major claims. It has preserved witness narratives and added occasional photographs, but rarely the original files, precise observation geometry, contemporaneous air-traffic records or independent technical analysis needed to move from “unidentified” to “extraordinary”. In several cases, later scrutiny weakened the UFO interpretation by revealing familiar objects or environmental causes.

That leaves Umbria neither a hidden hotspot nor an empty space on Italy’s UFO map. It is a region where a few genuinely puzzling observations survive alongside many examples of how unfamiliar lights, human expectation and incomplete evidence can turn an ordinary event—or an event that can no longer be reconstructed—into a lasting local mystery.

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
