## Introduction

The most useful surviving material consists of dated sighting catalogues, contemporary or later press reports, witness accounts and the Italian Air Force’s national reporting system. These sources show that people genuinely reported puzzling things. They also reveal serious limitations: many entries are brief summaries with no original testimony, radar record, photograph or investigation file attached. Several Marche sightings were later attributed to meteors, aircraft, chimney flames, signal rockets or space activity. The region therefore offers a good case study in how memorable UFO history can grow from a mixture of unresolved observations, ordinary objects seen under difficult conditions and stories that become stronger as they are retold.

## The October 1954 wave

The earliest major cluster recorded for Marche occurred in October 1954, during a wider European and Italian wave of flying-saucer reports. A catalogue maintained by the Italian National UFO Centre lists sightings on 19 October from Ancona, [Falconara]({{ 'falconara/' | relative_url }}), Jesi, Fabriano and Senigallia, where witnesses reportedly watched several cigar-shaped objects for an extended period. Further reports followed from Ascoli Piceno and Marotta.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/marche.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span></span></span>

The densest sequence came early on 25 October. Between approximately 5.30 and 6.30 in the morning, luminous spheres, cylinders, discs and objects with coloured trails were reported from numerous places, including Potenza Picena, Camerino, Filottrano, Fabriano, Pesaro, Ancona, Tolentino, Sassoferrato, Urbino, Jesi, Falconara and Fano. Several descriptions appear to refer to the same phenomenon moving across a wide area rather than to separate craft hovering over each town.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/marche.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span></span></span>

That geographical spread is important. When observers tens or hundreds of kilometres apart report a brilliant object within minutes of one another, an astronomical or high-altitude atmospheric event becomes more plausible than a low-flying vehicle. Many descriptions included a bright nucleus, a long coloured trail and rapid movement towards the horizon—features commonly associated with a fireball meteor. The catalogue itself occasionally records conventional possibilities, describing one Ascoli report as perhaps an aircraft, although it does not give a settled explanation for the morning sequence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/marche.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span></span></span>

One entry from Colcerasa is much more dramatic: a barrel-like object allegedly landed and three large-headed humanoids emerged. Yet the surviving catalogue provides only a short sentence and no names, original interviews, photographs, physical traces or contemporary investigation documents. It should therefore be treated as an intriguing piece of UFO folklore rather than evidence comparable with the multiple-observer luminous event earlier that day.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/marche.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span></span></span>

The [1954 wave]({{ '1954-wave-9ad069/' | relative_url }}) matters because it establishes a pattern repeated throughout Marche’s UFO history. A broad, possibly astronomical event generated many relatively simple reports, while a smaller number of extraordinary stories became attached to the same period. Without the underlying files, those two kinds of claim cannot safely be given equal weight.

## The Adriatic stories of 1978

Italy experienced an exceptional wave of UFO reporting in 1978. The volume of reports was significant enough that Prime Minister Giulio Andreotti assigned the Italian Air Force responsibility for collecting, checking and monitoring UFO notifications. The Air Force still describes this as the origin of its institutional role: reports are investigated, and cases for which no technical or natural cause can be identified may remain classified as unidentified.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Una volta terminati gli accertamenti, gli episodi vengono pubblicati alla voce Avvistamenti di questa pagina e, se non è stato possibile...</span></span></span>

In Marche, the best-known part of the 1978 story concerns the Adriatic coast. Later regional reporting says fishermen described strange lights or objects above and below the sea, sudden columns of water and unexplained radar contacts during the autumn. These claims have become central to the idea of an “Adriatic mystery” stretching southwards from Ancona towards San Benedetto del Tronto and the Abruzzo coast.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.tvcentromarche.it/2022/07/02/17283/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: tvcentromarche.it">[tvcentromarche.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">tvcentromarche.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">GIORNAT A MONDIALE UFO, OLTRE 500 AVVISTAMENTI NELLE MARCHE</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">GIORNAT A MONDIALE UFO, OLTRE 500 AVVISTAMENTI NELLE MARCHE</span></span></span>

The difficulty is that popular summaries often combine different witnesses, dates and types of event into one apparently coherent episode. A light seen above the horizon, a disturbance in the sea and an unidentified return on a fishing vessel’s radar are not automatically evidence of the same object. Radar near the sea can also be affected by waves, weather, anomalous propagation, vessel echoes and equipment limitations. Claims of radar confirmation are valuable only when the instrument type, time, position, raw display or operator record can be examined.

There was also genuine unusual marine activity in the Adriatic during 1978. On 21 June, months before the autumn UFO accounts, a large tsunami-like sea-level disturbance affected the central and southern Adriatic. Later scientific research classified it as a probable meteotsunami—a surge generated by atmospheric pressure waves rather than an earthquake. It does not directly explain every later report from Marche, but it demonstrates that sudden and alarming sea behaviour in the Adriatic can have rare natural causes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://jadran.izor.hr/~vilibic/meteotsunami/Vucetic-etal_PCE_2009.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: jadran.izor.hr">[jadran.izor.hr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">jadran.izor.hr</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Vucetic etal PCE 2009</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Great Adriatic flood of 21 June 1978 revisitedNovember 17, 2009 — by T Vucetic · 2009 · Cited by 59 — This paper describes an extraor...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 17, 2009</span></span></span>

These cases are more interesting than isolated reports because some include multiple witnesses, optical aids or an aviation connection. The coastal setting also creates predictable misidentification risks. Aircraft approaching or departing Falconara can appear nearly stationary when moving towards an observer. Fishing vessels, offshore lights, planets close to the horizon and distant illumination distorted by layers of warm and cool air can produce apparent movement or changes in shape. Human depth perception is poor over a dark sea, making it exceptionally difficult to distinguish a nearby small light from a powerful distant one.

This does not mean that every coastal report was explained. It means that phrases such as “entered the sea” may describe an object disappearing below the observer’s horizon rather than physically submerging. A convincing underwater-object claim would require independent observations from different positions, measured trajectories or sensor records that excluded this basic geometrical effect.

## Monte Conero: military reality and UFO legend

Monte Conero has become Marche’s best-known UFO location because dramatic scenery, restricted military areas and repeated reports from the surrounding coast reinforce one another. Stories about underground installations, tunnels, secret entrances and alien technology have led some writers to present the mountain as an Italian equivalent of Area 51. Even general descriptions of the site acknowledge that such claims are speculative rather than established facts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Conero" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Monte ConeroLe teorie più audaci parlano di una sorta di Area 51, per cui la base all&#x27;interno del monte avrebbe a che fare con forme di v...</span></span></span>

There is a real military association, and that alone can encourage suspicion. Military communications, surveillance facilities and restricted zones are easily incorporated into UFO narratives because the public naturally has incomplete information about their functions. Yet evidence that a site has a defence role is not evidence that it stores anomalous craft or is connected with unidentified lights.

The regional catalogue does contain reports from Monte Conero and nearby places. On 17 September 1986, a brown disc with a dome was reportedly seen at Monte Conero and later at Mazzangrugno; additional ovoid reports came from nearby Poggio and the wider coastal area that month. In later years, Sirolo, Numana and Portonovo continued to appear in sighting lists.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/avvistamenti/marche.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico Nazionalewww.centroufologiconazionale.net</span></span></span>

These reports justify treating the Conero coast as a regional hotspot in the descriptive sense: it generated repeated claims. “Hotspot” should not, however, be confused with proof of an unusual physical concentration. Popularity, dark-sky viewing, heavy seasonal tourism, aircraft routes, maritime traffic and an existing reputation for mystery can all increase reporting. Once a location is labelled a UFO zone, ambiguous lights are more likely to be noticed, interpreted as strange and reported.

According to the account given to ANSA by an independent investigator, the light hovered or manoeuvred at low apparent altitude for several minutes, then climbed rapidly and disappeared towards Pian dell’Elmo. ## What the official record can and cannot prove

The Italian Air Force provides the clearest official framework for evaluating Marche reports. Its role is to receive notifications, conduct technical checks and publish cases after assessment. It also provides a detailed reporting form asking witnesses about time, direction, apparent shape, colour, movement, weather and other observational circumstances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Una volta terminati gli accertamenti, gli episodi vengono pubblicati alla voce Avvistamenti di questa pagina e, se non è stato possibile...</span></span></span>

An official “unidentified” classification has a limited but important meaning. It indicates that the available inquiry did not establish a satisfactory technical or natural explanation. It does not certify extraterrestrial origin, advanced technology or even the presence of a solid object. A case can remain unidentified because the observation was genuinely anomalous, but also because the report arrived late, contained insufficient detail or could not be checked against complete records.

Private catalogues serve a different purpose. The National UFO Centre’s Marche chronology is valuable because it preserves dates, locations and brief summaries that might otherwise be forgotten. A local Marche group similarly distinguishes between cases it regards as rationally explained and those it considers unexplained. Neither classification can substitute for the underlying evidence, particularly when the public entry is only a few lines long.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ufologiamarche.altervista.org/segnalazioni.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufologiamarche.altervista.org">[ufologiamarche.altervista.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufologiamarche.altervista.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on altervista.org.</span></span></span>

## Modern sightings are often identified faster

A striking example appeared on 24 March 2025, when a luminous spiral was photographed from Marche and many other parts of Europe. Its large, structured form looked genuinely unusual, and social media rapidly filled with speculation. Local reporting soon connected it with a SpaceX Falcon 9 launch and the release of residual gas at high altitude.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cronacheancona.it/2025/03/25/spirale-luminosa-in-cielo-avvistamenti-anche-nelle-marche/544738/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cronacheancona.it">[Cronache Ancona]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cronacheancona.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on cronacheancona.it.</span></span></span>

Independent reporting outside Italy confirmed the explanation. The spiral followed the Falcon 9 launch of the NROL-69 mission from Cape Canaveral. Venting from the rotating upper stage produced an expanding cloud of frozen material that reflected sunlight while observers below were in darkness. Its simultaneous visibility across a huge part of Europe was precisely what would be expected from a phenomenon occurring hundreds of kilometres above Earth, not from a local object over Marche.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theguardian.com/world/2025/mar/25/alien-hopes-crash-to-earth-glowing-spiral-uk-spacex-rocket" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theguardian.com">[The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theguardian.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on theguardian.com.</span></span></span>

This case demonstrates what investigators lacked in many historical reports: photographs from widely separated locations, an exact time, orbital and launch information, and a known mechanism capable of reproducing the appearance. It also shows why modern skies may generate more—not fewer—UFO reports. Rocket stages, satellite trains, re-entries and fuel vents create unfamiliar displays that can be spectacular before tracking data identify them. Scientific studies have shown that artificial re-entries can be reconstructed from public video and instrument networks, allowing apparently mysterious lights to be linked to specific space hardware.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.01004" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

## The strongest assessment of Marche’s UFO history

Marche has a genuine history of unidentified-sighting reports, with three particularly important strands: the geographically broad luminous wave of October 1954, the poorly documented but culturally influential Adriatic stories of 1978, and the repeated coastal reports around Ancona and Monte Conero during the 1980s. These episodes matter because they connect the region’s landscape, fishing and aviation communities, military presence and local press culture.

The best-supported conclusion is not that all Marche cases were ordinary, nor that unresolved cases demonstrate extraterrestrial activity. It is that the evidence is uneven. Some reports were positively explained; many are too thinly documented to reassess; a smaller group contain potentially useful features such as multiple witnesses, prolonged observation or aviation experience but lack the primary records needed to make those features decisive.

A high-quality regional investigation would prioritise original witness statements, contemporary newspaper pages, Air Force case documents, radar logs, weather records, astronomical calculations and air-traffic information. It would also keep cases separate rather than combining every light, sea disturbance and rumoured radar contact into a single narrative. Modern scientific proposals for studying unidentified aerial phenomena emphasise this same principle: reliable conclusions require synchronised cameras, radar or range measurements, environmental sensors and independent corroboration, not testimony or a single image alone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18566" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on arxiv.org.</span></span></span>

On the evidence presently available, Marche’s most famous incidents belong in three different categories. The March 2025 spiral is convincingly explained. Many catalogue entries are weak or impossible to judge because the supporting documentation is absent. A limited number of older multi-witness cases remain unresolved in the ordinary sense of the word: their cause has not been established. That is enough to justify continued historical and archival interest, but not enough to turn regional mystery into confirmed extraordinary visitation.

    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
