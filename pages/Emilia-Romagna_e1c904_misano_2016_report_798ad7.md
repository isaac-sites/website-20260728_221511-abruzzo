## Introduction

The Misano Adriatico sighting of 15 August 2016 is useful because it shows exactly what an official “unidentified” label does, and does not, mean. A municipal police officer reportedly saw groups of pale, spheroidal objects over the Rimini coast in the early afternoon; the case later appeared in the Italian Air Force’s 2016 list of unidentified flying object reports. The Air Force record says the event could not be associated with known flight activity or known radiosonde activity, but it does not say that the objects were alien craft, advanced technology, or physically extraordinary in any proven sense.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/OVNI-2016.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Emilia-Romagna_e1c904_misano_2016_report_798ad7-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Misano 2016" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">
That distinction matters for [Emilia-Romagna]({{'what-really-happened-in-emilia/' | relative_url }})’s UFO history. Misano 2016 is not one of the region’s older folklore-heavy episodes, nor a mass flap on the scale of 1978. It is a modern, documented, coastal sighting that passed into an official national archive because a reported aerial event remained unmatched after the checks described by the Air Force’s OVNI procedure.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span>

## What the witness reportedly saw

The public account centres on Ferragosto, the Italian mid-August holiday, at Misano Adriatico on the Romagna coast. Local press reported that Luca Tamburini, an assistant chief in the municipal police of San Giovanni in Marignano, saw about twenty white spheres moving slowly in the sky near Bagno 69 on 15 August 2016. The same report said other people on the beach also saw the phenomenon, and that Tamburini photographed it, uploaded a video, and submitted documentation to the Carabinieri and the Italian Air Force.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/rimini/cronaca/ufo-misano-adriatico-sfere-4f722889" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ilrestodelcarlino.it">[Il Resto del Carlino]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ilrestodelcarlino.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il Resto del Carlino Ufo, 20 sfere bianche avvistate a Misano. The later Air Force entry gives the official catalogue version rather than the fuller newspaper narrative. It places the event at Porto Verde, Misano Adriatico, in the province of Rimini, at 14:34 local time. It describes “a group of objects” of spheroidal form: one white group and a second white and red-orange group. The listed speed is below 100 km/h, the motion is both straight and rotating, the direction is from south to north-east, and the altitude is estimated at about 8,000 metres above ground. The recorded conditions were clear sky, good visibility and moderate wind.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/OVNI-2016.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare</span></span></span>

There is a small but important difference between the local press version and the official summary. The press account emphasises “twenty white spheres”, beach witnesses and a visible “little fleet”. The Air Force table compresses the sighting into categories: place, time, shape, colour, speed, direction, motion, altitude, weather and reporting source. ## Why the case entered official records

Italy’s Air Force has been the institutional body for collecting, checking and monitoring OVNI reports since the wave of sightings in 1978. Its current public procedure asks citizens to use a reporting form and submit it through the Carabinieri. The Air Force says this allows a technical inquiry into possible links with human activity or natural phenomena, involving other competent bodies if needed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span>

The key point is the threshold for publication as an unidentified flying object. The Air Force states that, once checks are completed, cases are published in the “sightings” section and classified as unidentified when no technical or natural justification has been found. It describes the purpose of the activity as flight safety and national security, not as proof of any particular extraordinary origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span>

## What makes Misano different within Emilia-Romagna

Misano 2016 is significant for Emilia-Romagna because it is a recent official record from the region, not merely a local rumour. Many UFO stories circulate through newspapers, social media or specialist groups without ever becoming part of a formal state archive. This case did, and that gives it a firmer documentary footprint than a casual online sighting report.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/rimini/cronaca/ufo-misano-d645882a" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ilrestodelcarlino.it">[Il Resto del Carlino]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ilrestodelcarlino.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo misano d645882a</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ufo misano d645882a</span></span></span>

It is also a coastal daylight case. Many UFO reports are night lights, where distance, size and motion are especially hard to judge. Misano was reported in the early afternoon, under clear conditions, over a crowded summer coastal setting. That does not automatically make the observation reliable, but it changes the kind of problem investigators face. Instead of a single point of light in darkness, the reported issue was a group of pale objects in daytime sky, apparently moving slowly and visibly enough to attract attention.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ilrestodelcarlino.it/rimini/cronaca/ufo-misano-adriatico-sfere-4f722889" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ilrestodelcarlino.it">[Il Resto del Carlino]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ilrestodelcarlino.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il Resto del Carlino Ufo, 20 sfere bianche avvistate a Misano. Guarda il video</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il Resto del Carlino Ufo, 20 sfere bianche avvistate a Misano. Guarda il video</span></span></span>

The location matters as well. Misano Adriatico sits on the Adriatic tourist belt near Rimini, where summer skies can include aircraft, advertising activity, recreational balloons, drones, kites, reflective debris and unusual atmospheric visibility conditions. The Air Force’s unresolved label means its checks did not identify a known flight or radiosonde explanation; it does not mean every ordinary possibility was physically impossible.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span></span></span><img src="{{"/assets/images/Emilia-Romagna_e1c904_misano_2016_report_798ad7-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Misano 2016 illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{"/assets/images/Emilia-Romagna_e1c904_misano_2016_report_798ad7-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{"/assets/images/Emilia-Romagna_e1c904_misano_2016_report_798ad7-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">
## What the unresolved label does not prove

The most common misunderstanding is to treat “officially unidentified” as if it meant “officially extraordinary”. The Misano case does not support that leap. The Air Force’s own explanation of its OVNI process is careful: an episode is classified as unidentified when no technical or natural justification has been found after checks. ## Why Misano 2016 still matters

Misano 2016 matters because it is a clean example of the middle ground that dominates serious UFO history. It is neither a proven encounter with something exotic nor a neatly explained misidentification in the public record. It sits in the harder category: witnessed, reported, officially catalogued, and still not explained in the limited information released.

For Emilia-Romagna, that makes the case a useful marker. The region’s UFO history includes older waves, civilian catalogues, press cycles and modern misidentifications, but Misano shows how a recent case can pass through an official channel and remain unresolved without becoming a licence for overclaiming. It is precisely the kind of case where the phrase “unidentified” should be read carefully: it records a failure to identify, not a successful proof of the extraordinary.
