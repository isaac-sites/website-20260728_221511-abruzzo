## Introduction

[Aosta Valley]({{ 'why-aosta-valleys-ufo-record-looks-so/' | relative_url }})’s best-known UFO statistic is also its most misleading: local reporting has credited the region with **more than 80 sightings since 1947**, while Italian Air Force statistics listed only **two cases between 1972 and 2013**. The figures look irreconcilable, but they do not count the same thing. The larger total is a broad historical compilation assembled from press stories, witness accounts, photographs and specialist catalogues. The smaller total belongs to a formal state system in which reports reached the military through an official channel and underwent technical checks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.valledaostaglocal.it/2014/03/01/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-2/articolo/ufo-dal-1947-ad-oggi-circa-80-avvistamenti-in-valle.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: valledaostaglocal.it">[valledaostaglocal.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">valledaostaglocal.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in valle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AostaCronacaUFO: Dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in Valle - Valledaostaglocal.it...</span></span></span>



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/Aosta_Valley_ccb120_eighty_vs_two_cases_2aba79-overview.webp" | relative_url }}" alt="Overview image for Two Counts" loading="eager" decoding="sync" fetchpriority="high">

The disagreement therefore tells us more about record-keeping than about the number of mysterious objects over the Alps. It shows how a regional UFO chronology can grow through decades of retelling, while an official archive remains narrow because it applies different dates, procedures and admission rules. Neither figure should be treated as a count of confirmed extraordinary craft.

## How the figure of 80 reports emerged

The claim of “more than 80 sightings” appeared prominently in Aosta Valley coverage in March 2014. The article said that reports had been recorded from 1947 to 2013 and identified the latest as a photograph taken at La Thuile on 12 July 2013 by a visiting mother and daughter. It then presented a short chronology of earlier events, including a luminous trail seen in 1947, manoeuvring lights near the Rutor massif in 1977, elongated objects over a campsite in 1979 and a luminous body filmed by a television crew in 1985.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.valledaostaglocal.it/2014/03/01/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-2/articolo/ufo-dal-1947-ad-oggi-circa-80-avvistamenti-in-valle.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: valledaostaglocal.it">[AostaCronaca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">valledaostaglocal.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in valle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AostaCronacaUFO: Dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in Valle - Valledaostaglocal.it...</span></span></span>

That chronology illustrates the inclusive character of the larger number. Its entries cover very different kinds of material:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* observations made before Italy established its modern official reporting system;
* newspaper reports and retrospective recollections;
* photographs or films submitted to journalists or private researchers;
* events described as possible UFOs even when an ordinary explanation was suggested;
* separate witness reports that may or may not represent separate incidents.

</div>

The 1967 entry, for example, concerned fiery lights that the same local account said were probably fragments associated with a Soviet space launch. A strange light reported in December 1985 was described as possibly a bright meteor. Such cases can legitimately remain in a historical catalogue of reported sightings, because people did report something unusual, but they should not be counted as enduring mysteries without qualification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.valledaostaglocal.it/2014/03/01/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-2/articolo/ufo-dal-1947-ad-oggi-circa-80-avvistamenti-in-valle.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: valledaostaglocal.it">[AostaCronaca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">valledaostaglocal.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in valle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AostaCronacaUFO: Dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in Valle - Valledaostaglocal.it...</span></span></span>

There is also a basic provenance problem: the published article did not provide a case-by-case list of all 80 reports or explain precisely which archive produced that total. The reader can inspect several examples, but cannot audit the full number, check for duplicates or determine how many incidents were subsequently explained.

Other published totals reinforce that caution. A later report in *La Stampa*, citing the Italian Centre for UFO Studies, said that Aosta Valley had recorded **more than 50** sightings since 1947, not more than 80. A separate national catalogue produced by the Centro Ufologico Nazionale listed only **29 Aosta Valley cases** in its archive at the end of 2014.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lastampa.it/aosta/2014/08/25/news/il-monte-bianco-dei-misteri-tra-ufo-e-falsi-profeti-1.35626657" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lastampa.it">[La Stampa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lastampa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">La Stampa Il Monte Bianco dei misteri, tra Ufo e falsi profeti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La StampaIl Monte Bianco dei misteri, tra Ufo e falsi profeti - La Stampa...</span></span></span>

These figures need not prove that one compiler made an error. Private catalogues can differ because they use different sources, geographical boundaries and definitions of a case. One archive may include every press clipping; another may require a witness interview or fuller documentation. One may merge several reports of the same light, while another records them separately. The variation from 29 to more than 50 and more than 80 is itself evidence that the regional total is not a single standardised measurement.

## What the Air Force statistics include

Italy’s official system developed in response to the large national wave of reports in 1978, after which the government designated the Italian Air Force as the institution responsible for collecting, checking and monitoring UFO reports. Under the current procedure, a witness completes a reporting form and submits it to a Carabinieri station. The Air Force can then compare the account with known aviation activity, weather information, human-made objects and natural phenomena, consulting other bodies where necessary.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare - Ministero della DifesaSegnalazioni O.V.N.I., Oggetti Volanti Non Identificati. The official category is deliberately limited. Once checks are complete, an episode is classified as an unidentified flying object when investigators cannot establish a technical or natural explanation from the information available. That wording does not certify that the object was an alien vehicle, nor does it establish that every detail of the witness account was physically accurate. It means only that the investigation did not produce an adequate identification.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Aeronautica Militare OVNI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI - Aeronautica Militare - Ministero della DifesaSegnalazioni O.V.N.I., Oggetti Volanti Non Identificati. The national statistics publicised in 2014 covered 445 cases from 1972 to 2013. They assigned 53 to Lazio, 43 to Tuscany, 36 each to Lombardy and Campania, and only two to Aosta Valley. Molise, with one, was the sole region below it.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rainews.it/archivio-rainews/articoli/ufo-avvistamenti-segnalazioni-italia-aeronautica-218a7f1a-d128-4494-b464-066f409c5400.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rainews.it">[RaiNews]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rainews.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dal 1972, 445 avvistamenti di Ufo in Italia29 Mar 2014 — Nel dettaglio, la classifica delle regioni per numero di avvistamenti ved...</span></span></span>

At least one of Aosta Valley’s official entries was associated with the national rise in reports during 2010. Contemporary coverage stated that the Air Force recorded 22 Italian cases that year, including one from Aosta Valley. ## Why the totals diverge

The gap is produced by several overlapping differences rather than one hidden contradiction.

**The periods are not identical.** The regional figure begins in 1947. The Air Force comparison begins in 1972, and the formal institutional role was established after the 1978 wave. Many incidents used to build the larger total therefore pre-date the system against which they are being compared.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.valledaostaglocal.it/2014/03/01/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-2/articolo/ufo-dal-1947-ad-oggi-circa-80-avvistamenti-in-valle.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: valledaostaglocal.it">[AostaCronaca]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">valledaostaglocal.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in valle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AostaCronacaUFO: Dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in Valle - Valledaostaglocal.it...</span></span></span>

## Why both figures can be technically correct

The claim that Aosta Valley accumulated more than 80 UFO reports can be correct in the limited sense that a compiler found more than 80 accounts, clippings, images or testimonies fitting a broad definition. The statement that the Air Force recorded two regional cases from 1972 to 2013 can also be correct because it refers to a much narrower official series.

A useful way to read the numbers is:

FigureWhat it most plausibly representsWhat it does not establishMore than 80A broad regional chronology of reported unusual aerial events since 1947Eighty verified or still-unexplained objectsMore than 50A different specialist or journalistic compilation using its own inclusion rulesA complete official count29Cases held in one private national UFO archive by the end of 2014All reports ever made in Aosta ValleyTwoAosta Valley cases in the Air Force’s 1972–2013 regional statisticsEvery sighting witnessed or mentioned in the region

The most important word is therefore not “sightings” but **dataset**. Before comparing totals, readers need to know who collected the reports, the period covered, whether explained cases were retained, how duplicates were handled and whether formal submission was required.

## What the clash reveals about Aosta Valley

The numerical dispute weakens any simple claim that Aosta Valley experienced an exceptional concentration of unexplained aircraft. The region certainly developed a sizeable body of UFO stories, including several visually striking incidents. Yet the supporting record is fragmented, and even private organisations have produced markedly different totals. The official archive, meanwhile, shows that very few cases entered or survived the institutional process during the stated period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.valledaostaglocal.it/2014/03/01/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita-2/articolo/ufo-dal-1947-ad-oggi-circa-80-avvistamenti-in-valle.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: valledaostaglocal.it">[valledaostaglocal.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">valledaostaglocal.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in valle</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AostaCronacaUFO: Dal 1947 ad oggi circa 80 avvistamenti in Valle - Valledaostaglocal.it...</span></span></span>

That does not mean the other reports were false. Some may never have been formally submitted; some may have lacked enough information for investigation; others may have been ordinary phenomena recorded because they initially appeared mysterious. The available totals cannot tell us how those possibilities divide.

The fairest conclusion is that **Aosta Valley has a rich informal sighting history but a very small official case record**. The “80 versus two” contrast is not evidence that the Air Force disproved or concealed 78 incidents. Nor does the larger number override the official files. It is a comparison between a broad cultural archive and a selective investigative archive—two records built for different purposes, with different thresholds and different meanings.
