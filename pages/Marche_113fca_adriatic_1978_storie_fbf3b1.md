## Introduction

The 1978 Adriatic mystery was not one clearly documented incident but a cluster of reports that later became fused into a single regional legend. Fishermen along the [Marche]({{ 'how-marches-ufo-legends-took-shape/' | relative_url }})–Abruzzo coast described lights over or near the sea, sudden columns of water, disturbed surfaces and unreliable instruments. The most important official-looking episode came on 9 November, when five men aboard the Pescara harbour authority patrol boat CP 2018 reported a red light rising vertically from the sea off Silvi before disappearing within seconds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/faq/ufo-govari.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[centroufologiconazionale.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale Subject9 Novembre ore 21:00</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico NazionaleSubject9 Novembre ore 21:00 - L&#x27;equipaggio della motovedetta CP 2018 della Capitaneria di Porto di Pescara che...</span></span></span>

These stories matter to Marche because Ancona formed the northern point of what later writers called the “Adriatic Triangle”, usually drawn between Ancona, Pescara and the Gran Sasso. Yet the surviving evidence does not demonstrate that all the reported lights, water disturbances and radar tales had one cause. The strongest conclusion is more modest: during Italy’s exceptional UFO wave of 1978, several poorly documented maritime events were gathered into a powerful coastal narrative whose later versions became more dramatic and more unified than the available contemporary record justifies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/en/2023/01/12/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[difesa.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI12 Jan 2023 — Following the wave of Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) sightings in 1978, the then Prime Minister G...</span></span></span>

## Lights, water columns and fishermen’s accounts

The maritime reports appear to have intensified in October and November 1978, when national interest in UFOs was already unusually high. The Italian National UFO Centre later estimated that the news media reported roughly 557 sightings during 1978, rising to about 650 when January and February 1979 were included. That volume created ideal conditions for unrelated observations to be interpreted as parts of the same wave. The Italian Air Force confirms that the 1978 surge led Prime Minister Giulio Andreotti to assign it responsibility for collecting, checking and monitoring UFO reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/faq/ufo-govari.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale Subject9 Novembre ore 21:00</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico NazionaleSubject9 Novembre ore 21:00 - L&#x27;equipaggio della motovedetta CP 2018 della Capitaneria di Porto di Pescara che...</span></span></span>

The most repeated coastal claim concerns enormous columns or fountains of water rising from an otherwise calm Adriatic. Later accounts place these events mainly between Pescara, Martinsicuro and the waters towards San Benedetto del Tronto. Descriptions vary markedly: some sources speak of columns about ten metres wide, while others give heights of twenty or thirty metres and add foaming water, dark shapes or violet lights inside them.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rete8.it/cronaca/456221855abruzzo-ufo-in-adriatico-dopo-40-anni-il-mistero-resta/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rete8.it">[rete8.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rete8.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Abruzzo: Ufo in Adriatico?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dopo 40 anni il mistero resta15 Mar 2018 — Dalle acque del mare, come raccontavano gli stessi pescatori, venivano fuori colonne d&#x27;acqua d...</span></span></span>

That variation is important. A stable, well-recorded physical event would normally retain reasonably consistent measurements, locations, times and witness details. Here, dimensions and visual features change from one retelling to another, and most accessible accounts do not provide named witnesses, signed statements, photographs, vessel logs or precise coordinates. The water-column story may preserve genuine observations, but its most spectacular details are supported chiefly by later journalism and UFO-oriented summaries rather than by readily available primary documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ilmartino.it/2015/12/1978-2015-il-triangolo-delladriatico-la-verita-sugli-avvistamenti-alieni-che-interessarono-anche-la-cittadina-di-martinsicuro/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ilmartino.it">[ilmartino.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ilmartino.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on ilmartino.it.</span></span></span>

One comparatively specific witness story concerns a San Benedetto fisherman identified in later accounts as Pallanza. He reportedly saw a brilliant red globe several hundred metres from his boat on 7 November. The light was said to remain alongside the vessel as he changed course towards land. This is more useful than a vague report of “strange lights” because it supplies a date, occupation, approximate distance and sequence of movement. Even so, the surviving public version appears in retrospective UFO material rather than an original interview transcript, so details such as duration, bearing, weather and comparison with other boats’ lights remain uncertain.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.crprato.it/sito/index.php?Itemid=329&amp;id=2370&amp;option=com_content&amp;view=article" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: crprato.it">[crprato.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">crprato.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on crprato.it.</span></span></span>

Other stories added malfunctioning compasses, radio interference, sudden fog and radar contacts. These claims became central to the legend because they implied more than visual misidentification: they suggested that an unknown presence physically affected vessels. Yet the evidential basis is especially weak. Later articles often repeat that instruments “went mad” without identifying the boats, equipment, repair findings or recorded errors. Some versions even differ over whether radar detected unexplained objects or failed to detect anything at all.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.pescarapescara.it/2017/12/16/ufo-a-pescara-la-storia-dimenticata/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pescarapescara.it">[pescarapescara.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pescarapescara.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO a Pescara: la storia dimenticata del 197816 Dec 2017 — Colonne d&#x27;acqua che si sollevano dall&#x27;Adriatico senza apparente...</span></span></span>

The most defensible reading is therefore that fishermen reported several kinds of puzzling experience, but that only a few reports can be separated from the folklore that accumulated around them. Lights over water, surface disturbance and temporary equipment problems are not inherently connected. They require different evidence and may have had different causes.

## The CP 2018 patrol-boat sighting

The patrol-boat incident of 9 November is the clearest individual episode because it involved a harbour authority crew rather than an anonymous witness. Around 20:45 or 21:00, CP 2018 was reportedly about four nautical miles from the coast near Silvi. Five crew members, including commander Nello Di Valentino, saw a pale red light rise from the sea roughly 800 metres ahead or to starboard. It climbed vertically to an estimated height of about 200 metres and disappeared after approximately four seconds.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/faq/ufo-govari.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[centroufologiconazionale.net]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale Subject9 Novembre ore 21:00</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico NazionaleSubject9 Novembre ore 21:00 - L&#x27;equipaggio della motovedetta CP 2018 della Capitaneria di Porto di Pescara che...</span></span></span>

The initial description reportedly compared the object to a red rocket or flare. That comparison is significant because it offers an ordinary maritime explanation rather than presenting the observation immediately as a structured craft. According to a later reproduction of the contemporary newspaper account, the crew searched the area but found no vessel that could be identified as the source. Di Valentino was also said to have noted that the light did not descend slowly beneath a parachute in the manner of some distress flares.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://e-cronaca.blogspot.com/2020/06/e-se-gli-alieni-di-san-benedetto.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: e-cronaca.blogspot.com">[e-cronaca.blogspot.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">e-cronaca.blogspot.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">E se gli alieni di San Benedetto fossero dei missili?26 Jun 2020 — Il 9 novembre di quello stesso anno, dalla motovedetta CP 2018...</span></span></span>

The episode remains unresolved in the limited sense that no confirmed source was identified. It is not, however, strong evidence that an object emerged physically from beneath the sea. The language used in the reported telegram described a light rising “from the sea”, which may mean that its visible path began at the horizon or waterline. At night, observers commonly have difficulty judging whether a distant light originates on the surface, just beyond the horizon or in low cloud.

The reported absence of a radar contact does not strengthen the extraordinary interpretation. A flare, small rocket, distant aircraft light or brief luminous phenomenon might produce little or no useful return on a patrol boat’s marine radar. Radar is designed primarily to display solid targets with suitable size, material, range and geometry; failing to register a four-second light does not prove that the phenomenon lacked a conventional source.

Later versions complicate the story by adding a radar blackout or strong interference in the same area. Some reports present the malfunction as nearly simultaneous with the sighting, while others treat it as a later episode. The publicly accessible summaries do not provide the patrol boat’s maintenance record, radar display log, fault report or original harbour authority investigation. ## How separate reports became one narrative

The label “Adriatic Triangle” gave the events a memorable geographical form. By linking Ancona, Pescara and the Gran Sasso, writers could combine reports from Marche waters, the Abruzzo coast and the mountain interior into one apparent zone of activity. This framing made the story regionally important, but it was an interpretative construction rather than proof that the incidents followed a common route or mechanism.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rete8.it/cronaca/456221855abruzzo-ufo-in-adriatico-dopo-40-anni-il-mistero-resta/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rete8.it">[rete8.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rete8.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Abruzzo: Ufo in Adriatico?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dopo 40 anni il mistero resta15 Mar 2018 — Dalle acque del mare, come raccontavano gli stessi pescatori, venivano fuori colonne d&#x27;acqua d...</span></span></span>

Several forces helped separate reports merge.

**A national sighting wave.** Hundreds of UFO stories were circulating across Italy. Witnesses and journalists therefore had an established language for interpreting an unfamiliar light as part of a larger phenomenon rather than as an isolated maritime puzzle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.net/faq/ufo-govari.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.net">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.net</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale Subject9 Novembre ore 21:00</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico NazionaleSubject9 Novembre ore 21:00 - L&#x27;equipaggio della motovedetta CP 2018 della Capitaneria di Porto di Pescara che...</span></span></span>

**Repeated press imagery.** Water columns, glowing spheres, boiling seas and failed compasses formed a vivid package. Once these motifs appeared together in newspapers and magazines, later summaries repeated them as if they described a single continuous event. A national retrospective in the *Corriere della Sera*, for example, condensed the story into fishermen seeing towering water columns and beams of light during the night of 14–15 October, followed by the patrol-boat report in November.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.corriere.it/tecnologia/cards/dall-omaha-ufo-firenze-piu-famosi-avvistamenti-oggetti-volanti-non-identificati/ondata-italiana-78.shtml" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: corriere.it">[Corriere della Sera]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">corriere.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ondata italiana 78.shtml</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Corriere della SeraUfo, l&#x27;ondata italiana del &#x27;7817 May 2021 — Nella notte fra il 14 e il 15 ottobre i pescatori pescaresi vedono colonne...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2021</span></span></span>

**Geographical compression.** Reports from Pescara, Silvi, Martinsicuro and San Benedetto were often treated as occurring in one compact maritime area. From the perspective of regional folklore, that seems reasonable. For investigation, however, differences of tens of kilometres, several weeks and changing weather conditions matter greatly.

**Escalation through retelling.** Early descriptions of a rocket-like red light later became an object emerging from the water at extraordinary speed. Reports of disturbed water became vast columns containing dark bodies or violet illumination. Claims that radar saw nothing were joined by claims that radar malfunctioned or detected mysterious targets.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.pescarapescara.it/2017/12/16/ufo-a-pescara-la-storia-dimenticata/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pescarapescara.it">[pescarapescara.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pescarapescara.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO a Pescara: la storia dimenticata del 197816 Dec 2017 — Colonne d&#x27;acqua che si sollevano dall&#x27;Adriatico senza apparente...</span></span></span>

**Association with unrelated tragedy.** Some later narratives begin with the sinking of a fishing vessel called *Francesco Padre* and connect the deaths of two fishermen with the onset of the mystery. This claim is difficult to assess from the available record and should not be used as evidence of anomalous activity without the original casualty investigation, medical findings and correct vessel documentation. Retrospective sources repeat striking details about the victims, but those details are not supported by accessible [official records]({{ 'official-records/' | relative_url }}) in the material examined here.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.crprato.it/sito/index.php?Itemid=329&amp;id=2370&amp;option=com_content&amp;view=article" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: crprato.it">[crprato.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">crprato.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on crprato.it.</span></span></span>

This process does not require deliberate invention. Fishermen may sincerely have reported unusual lights or water behaviour, journalists may have accurately quoted parts of their testimony, and later writers may have tried to assemble a coherent chronology. The problem is that narrative coherence can grow even when the underlying events are unrelated.

## Ordinary causes do not need to explain everything at once

No single conventional explanation neatly accounts for every claim, but that is not evidence that one extraordinary object caused them all. Because the reports differ in type and date, the most realistic approach is to consider several possible causes.

Red lights rising from the waterline could include maritime distress flares, signal rockets, military illumination devices or lights launched beyond the observer’s visible horizon. The CP 2018 crew’s own comparison with a rocket makes this category especially relevant, even though the patrol did not identify a launching vessel.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rete8.it/cronaca/378quando-gli-ufo-invasero-labruzzo-ottobre-78/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rete8.it">[Rete8]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rete8.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">378quando gli ufo invasero labruzzo ottobre 78</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">1978: quando gli UFO &quot;invasero&quot; l&#x27;Abruzzo31 Oct 2015 — Episodio avvenuto in mare il 9 novembre, poco prima delle 21, alla presenza d...</span></span></span>

Lights apparently pacing fishing boats could have been other vessels, aircraft, stars or planets seen through moving cloud, or reflections distorted by haze and darkness. At sea, a witness often lacks fixed reference points, making distance and motion difficult to judge. A light that appears to follow a turning boat may actually be far away.

Water columns demand a different set of possibilities. Waterspouts, localised wind effects, breaking waves, underwater gas release, marine activity or human operations could produce unusual surface behaviour. However, the reports contain too little meteorological and positional information to select confidently among them. Claims of perfectly calm conditions also require contemporary weather observations rather than memory or later summaries.

Military activity has repeatedly been suggested because the Adriatic was strategically important during the Cold War. Missiles, naval exercises and aircraft could explain some lights and sounds. Yet a military explanation also needs evidence: exercise notices, unit records, radar tracks or declassified operational documents. In their absence, “secret tests” can become as unfalsifiable as extraterrestrial claims.

Instrument failures may have been ordinary faults, interference from nearby transmitters, power problems or operating limitations. To argue that an external object affected several boats, investigators would need synchronised records showing the same disturbance at the same time and place. The available public accounts do not provide that level of correlation.

## What records would be needed to connect them

The central question is not whether each witness was honest, but whether the events can be shown to belong to one physical sequence. That would require records far more precise than the summaries now commonly circulated.

The most valuable missing material would include:

* the original CP 2018 telegram, patrol log and crew statements;

<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* harbour authority records showing the patrol route and subsequent search;
* radar maintenance and fault reports from CP 2018 and nearby vessels;
* fishing-boat logbooks with exact times, coordinates and crew lists;
* contemporary weather, visibility and sea-state observations;
* distress-flare reports and maritime safety communications;
* military and civil aviation records for the relevant hours;
* original newspaper interviews rather than later paraphrases;
* photographs, film or independent observations from separated locations.

</div>

These records would allow investigators to test whether the 7 November fisherman’s light and the 9 November patrol sighting occurred along the same bearing, whether equipment failures were simultaneous, and whether reported water columns coincided with unusual weather or maritime activity. Without those connections, the cases remain a collection rather than a chain.

The Italian Air Force’s later UFO archive is important for understanding the official framework created after the 1978 wave, but its publicly described role does not by itself confirm that the Adriatic stories were investigated as one case. The fact that the government established a reporting system shows that the volume of public concern was real; it does not establish the nature of any particular sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aeronautica.difesa.it/en/2023/01/12/ovni/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aeronautica.difesa.it">[Aeronautica Militare]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aeronautica.difesa.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Aeronautica MilitareOVNI12 Jan 2023 — Following the wave of Unidentified Flying Object (UFO) sightings in 1978, the then Prime Minister G...</span></span></span>

## Did one mystery unite the reports?

The available evidence does not show that one object or phenomenon united the 1978 Adriatic reports. The CP 2018 red light is a reasonably specific unresolved observation, although a flare-like or other short-lived luminous source remains plausible. The fishermen’s lights may contain several genuine but independent sightings. The great water columns and widespread electromagnetic effects are much less secure because their details are inconsistent and their primary documentation is difficult to locate.

Within Marche’s UFO history, the story matters less as proof of a hidden underwater presence than as an example of how a regional flap forms. Ancona’s inclusion in the “triangle” connected Marche to a wider Adriatic narrative, while the experiences of fishing communities gave the story local authority and human immediacy. Press repetition then turned a sequence of scattered reports into one of central Italy’s most durable UFO legends.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.rete8.it/cronaca/456221855abruzzo-ufo-in-adriatico-dopo-40-anni-il-mistero-resta/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rete8.it">[rete8.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rete8.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Abruzzo: Ufo in Adriatico?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dopo 40 anni il mistero resta15 Mar 2018 — Dalle acque del mare, come raccontavano gli stessi pescatori, venivano fuori colonne d&#x27;acqua d...</span></span></span>

Later reporting has preserved but has not materially strengthened the case. It has supplied names, approximate distances and reconstructed quotations, yet it has also introduced larger measurements, additional malfunctions and stronger claims of objects emerging from beneath the sea. Until original logs, witness statements and technical records are recovered, the 1978 Adriatic mystery is best classified as a mixed and weakly connected cluster: historically significant, partly unresolved, but not demonstrated to have had a single extraordinary cause.
